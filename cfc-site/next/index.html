How it works

  1. 01

    I ride.

    10,000 miles in 2027, all of them on the bike. Every ride logs to Strava and lands on this page within the hour.

  2. 02

    You pledge.

    A few cents a mile, or a flat amount. Nothing changes hands until the year is over.

  3. 03

    You decide.

    At year-end, the board votes. The winning orgs get every dollar. Queer communities, every mile.

The pledge

Get on the board.

Free to join. No card, no amount today. You put up a few cents a mile, or a flat number, and settle up when the year is done.

At year-end the people on the board vote. Whatever they pick gets all of it. I don’t keep a cent.

You’re on the board. See you out there.

    The story

    Queer in the field. Miles where my mouth is.

    I came back from a place a lot of people don’t. Queer in a field that didn’t always leave room for it, and I stayed anyway. Now the miles go to the communities that kept people like me alive.
