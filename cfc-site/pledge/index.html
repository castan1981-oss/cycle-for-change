    Questions

    Everything people ask.

    The money

    Do I have to pay now?
    No. You pledge now and settle up in January 2028, one email, one link. No card until then.
    What does a pledge cost?
    You pick. At 1¢ a mile it’s $100 for the whole year; at 5¢ it’s $500. Or name a flat number, any size. You can cap it, too. Same deal.
    Where does the money go?
    Direct to the orgs, 100% of it. The people on the board vote which ones. I don’t keep a cent.
    Does any of it go to Robert?
    No. Not a dollar, not for the bike, not for the kit. The money never passes through me.
    How do I pay, and to whom?
    In January 2028 you get one email with the final miles, your number, and a link to give straight to the org that won the vote.
    Is it tax deductible?
    You give directly to the org, so it works the way any gift to them does. They send the receipt.
    What if you don’t make 10,000?
    You pay for the miles I rode. Every one of them is on Strava, and the count on this page updates within the hour.
    Can I change or cancel my pledge?
    Yes, any time before the year ends. Send the form again with the same name and I’ll keep the latest, or reply to any mile update.
    Can a team or a company pledge?
    Yes. One name on the board, any amount. If you want a logo on the kit, reply to a mile update and we’ll talk.

    The ride

    Can I ride with you?
    Yes. Six dates on the 2027 calendar so far, and every ride in the directory is open to anyone.
    What are ride days?
    The day before each of my six 2027 events, an easy open ride in that town for anyone on the board. No-drop, no sign-up fee. Where and when goes out by email a few weeks ahead.
    Do I have to be queer to be part of this?
    No. Anyone can pledge, anyone can ride. The money goes to queer-serving orgs. That’s the point.
    Can I change my vote?
    Any time before December 31, 2027. Come back to this page; your pick is remembered in your browser.
