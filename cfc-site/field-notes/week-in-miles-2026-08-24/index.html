Week in Miles: Aug 17–24

Four rides, 136.1 miles, 3,349 feet of climbing across Phoenix. Two cool desert mornings to start the week — then 113°F came back and reminded everyone what August is actually for.

Weekly cycling miles in Phoenix heat: the numbers

Monday and Tuesday were the gift. Low of 58°F, high of 85°F — that's not Phoenix summer, that's borrowed weather from somewhere better. The Monday ride went out partly cloudy with almost no wind. 19.3 mph average. 169 watts. The legs were there, the road was quiet, and for two hours it felt like a different city.

Tuesday pushed it into a lunch start — 11am, overcast, 87°F by then. Still 34 miles, still under 87°F. A little slower, which makes sense. Two solid days back to back. 71 miles in the first two days of the week.

Friday the bill came due. The low was 89°F. The high was 113.7°F. The ride title — "Craving a cool morning" — tells you everything. Out before 7am, still 35 miles, still almost 1,000 feet of climbing. Slower, 16.8 mph. Makes sense when the air is that heavy. Saturday started at 5:48am with 19 mph winds and a forecasted 112°F. Named it "Hot." That's about right. 30 miles and done.
