Week in Miles: July 28–August 3, 2026

Four rides. 96.5 miles. 3,737 feet of climbing. Three of them launched before 7am to stay ahead of Phoenix heat that hit 112°F by afternoon.

The overnight lows this week were 87°F, 91°F, 90°F. Those are the lows. The highs were 105°F, 110°F, 112°F on three straight days. July in Phoenix does not negotiate.

Three of the four rides started before 7am. That's what you do when the afternoon is going to spend hours above 110°F — you go early, or you rearrange the week.

Tuesday got named "Struggle bus." That's an honest title. The high was 110°F and the overnight low was 90.6°F — meaning it never actually cooled down between Monday and Tuesday. The air at 6:36am was still carrying the previous day's heat. 35.5 miles, 1,240 feet of climbing, 2h 18m. Finished before the worst of it, but that doesn't make it feel light.

Wednesday was the hottest day — 111.9°F high. Another early start, 36.7 miles, nearly the same pace as Tuesday. A few more watts. It's strange how the numbers hold steady when the conditions are working against you. The body adapts, or it gets convincing about how it is.

Friday was 1.4 miles. Not a typo. Some days that's the ride. The heat had dropped — 92°F high — but 1.4 miles is what got logged. The pledge counts them all the same.

96.5 miles this week. 3,737 feet of climbing. The 7,500-mile total doesn't care about the temperature — the miles stack regardless.
