161 Miles in Phoenix Summer Heat

Five morning rides, 161 miles, 5,187 feet of climbing — Phoenix running 103–111°F every day this week, August 3–10.

All five rides were early starts. Out by 6:30 AM or before. That's not ambition — that's how you get 35 miles done when the overnight low doesn't drop below 87°F and the afternoon will hit 111°F.

Overcast every day. That sounds like relief. It isn't. Not in August, not in Phoenix. The cloud cover traps heat from the day before. No sun glare, but no cooling either — the sky just holds everything in and the monsoon edge keeps the humidity higher than it has any right to be.

Aug 5 was the short day. One hour, 16 miles. Sometimes the schedule gives you one hour and that's what you take.

Aug 8 was the standout. Almost 40 miles, 1,358 feet of climbing, and an overnight low of 95°F. That low matters. When the ground doesn't cool down, the morning air doesn't either. By 8 AM you're already working against accumulated heat. Still put in the miles.
