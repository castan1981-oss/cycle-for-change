Phoenix Cycling in August

Four rides, Aug 10–17. 142.6 miles, 5,820 feet of climbing — all before 9 a.m., because by 9 a.m. Phoenix in August doesn't negotiate.

Phoenix in August means one thing: you ride early or you don't ride. All four of these were out the door before 7:30 a.m. Monday's "kinda cool morning" hit 102.8°F by afternoon. That's the joke. That's also just August here.

Wednesday was the standout. "Monsoon Sassoon" — light drizzle, 16-mph gusts, actual weather by Phoenix standards. The monsoon pattern had been building all week. A little moisture in the air changes things. Not comfortable, exactly, but different. The roads were quiet. That smell of rain on warm desert pavement doesn't last long, so you take it while it's there.

Sunday was the climbing day. 38.4 miles and 2,159 feet of gain — more than a third of the week's total climb in one ride. By the time the sun cleared the ridge it was already 86°F. You work with what the calendar gives you. Four rides, 142.6 miles, 5,820 feet — all of it going toward the 7,500-mile pledge for queer communities. The number moves. That's the point.
