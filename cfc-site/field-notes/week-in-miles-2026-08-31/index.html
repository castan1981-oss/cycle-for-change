Week in Miles: Aug 25–31, 2026

Seven rides, 244.9 miles, 8,311 feet of climbing — Phoenix in late August, when the mercury sat above 110°F for five straight days and the monsoon finally showed up by Friday. Every mile counted toward the 7,500-mile pledge.

Monday through Thursday the high never dropped below 110°F. The sky stayed overcast — that's the monsoon lid: cloud cover that blocks the sun but traps the humidity underneath. No shade benefit, no temperature relief. Rides start at 5:30 a.m. or they don't happen. Even then, the air is already 90-something by the time you're turning back.

Saturday was the standout. "Cooling" — 47.7 miles, 2,707 feet of climbing, heavy showers rolling through. 106°F with rain is still hot. But after five days above 110°F it registers as a different thing. The climbing on that ride was more than the other six days combined. Good effort. The name fits.

Sunday closed the week on the mountain bike. 19.4 miles, drizzle, 90.7°F — practically a cool morning by August standards. All seven rides add up to 244.9 miles and 8,311 feet of climbing toward the 7,500-mile pledge. The goal is to log every one of those miles by the end of 2027. This week moved the needle.
