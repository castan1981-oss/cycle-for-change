Week in Miles — Cycling Phoenix, July 21–27, 2026

Five rides, 174.3 miles, 4,655 feet of climbing — all on the bike, all in Phoenix, all before the heat made being outside a bad idea. High temperatures ranged from 102°F on Monday to 115°F by midweek. Zero rain all week.

Five rides. 174.3 miles. Phoenix summer heat at its peak.

The heat was the story this week. Monday's 102° high felt easy by comparison. Wednesday hit 115.4°. Thursday matched it at 115.2°. Every ride started before 7 a.m. That's not preference. That's physics. You do not ride in Phoenix in late July past 9 a.m. and expect to function the rest of the day.

The bigger climbing came early. Monday's ride — the one that got logged as three bike emojis — put up 1,165 feet and the highest average wattage of the week at 125.9 W. Tuesday added another 1,122. Wednesday was the fastest at 16.3 mph, which is odd given it was also the hottest day, but the route was flatter and the legs were still fresh. Thursday brought 20.2 mph wind on top of 115° air — that's the kind of headwind that makes a 34-mile ride feel considerably longer.
