Cycle for the Cause

The Northeast’s big one: a three-day ride in and out of New York City, benefiting the LGBT Community Center’s HIV/AIDS programs. Decades old and deeply loved.

While California had AIDS/LifeCycle, the East Coast had — and still has — Cycle for the Cause. It’s the NYC LGBT Community Center’s three-day ride, out of New York City and back: September 24 to 26, 2027. It used to be a 275-mile ride into Manhattan; now it starts and ends in the city. It funds the Center’s HIV prevention, testing, and support services.

The Cycle for the Cause route

Three days of real Northeast riding: rolling farmland, river valleys, the kind of punchy climbs California riders pretend don’t hurt. Fully supported — rest stops, SAG vehicles, gear trucks, two hotel nights and all meals covered. Registration is $95; the minimum is $3,000, or $2,000 if you’re 18 to 24.

The culture

This is the ride East Coast queer cyclists build their summer around. Teams come back year after year; the fundraising leaderboard gets competitive in the best way. If you’re in New York, Boston, Philly, or DC, this is your home ride.

First multi-day? Read the first-timer’s guide and start fundraising early — a team makes both the miles and the minimum easier.
