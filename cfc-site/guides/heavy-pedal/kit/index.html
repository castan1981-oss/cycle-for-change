Heavy Pedal kit: what they make, what it costs, how it fits

The Heavy Pedal sells its own road and gravel kit online and from its shop in Phoenix. Most jerseys cost $120–$135 and most bibs $145–$165. The $100 Collection is the cheap way in: a $40–$50 Attack jersey and a $50 Attack bib. Their product pages describe a slim race cut, so size up if you like it looser.

From The Heavy Pedal’s own product and policy pages, read Oct 23, 2026. We haven’t worn it, and we’re not connected to them. Prices move and sizes sell out; their site is the price that counts.

Beyond that: socks, “Elements” pieces, lifestyle tees, MTB jerseys, button-ups and gift cards.

The $100 kit

“$100 Kit” in their menu is the $100 Collection, the Attack line. Jerseys are $40–$50 and bibs $50, sold one at a time, so a jersey and a bib come to about $100. It’s the same race cut as the rest of the range, sized XS to 5XL on the jersey. Sizes sell out; on Oct 23, 2026 some Attack pieces were gone in most sizes.

How Heavy Pedal kit fits

  • Slim. The jerseys are cut for racing. The Attack page says to size up for a looser fit. The Blox bib page says it runs slim.
  • Sizes. XS to XXXL on most pieces, to 5XL on the Attack jersey.
  • The chart. Each product page has a size guide (it’s a picture, so measure yourself and read it on their page).
  • If it doesn’t fit. They exchange or refund unworn pieces within 30 days. Or go to the shop at 4700 N. Central Ave. #116 in Phoenix and see the sizes in person.

Custom team kit

For teams, clubs, shops, brands and events. The minimum is 10 pieces, and they quote about six weeks from an approved design to your door. Bring your own design, or bring an idea and they’ll draw it. They can also set up a team shop where your riders pre-order, so you don’t collect sizes and money yourself. Start on their custom page or at sales@theheavypedal.com.

Shipping, returns, warranty, crashes

  • Shipping. Free in the US over $60. Free internationally over $160; duties and import taxes are the buyer’s.
  • Returns. In the US, back within 30 days of delivery, unused, tags on, for a refund; after that, store credit. 45 days outside the US. You pay the return postage. Sale items are final.
  • Warranty. 9 months, for manufacturing defects: a chamois coming unstitched, a broken zipper, broken thread. Not crash damage, saddle chafing, wear from sweat, or washing against the care label.
  • Crash replacement. A discount on a new piece after you go down. Their warranty page says 40% off in the first 6 months, 25% after that and 15% after 2 years. A note elsewhere on the site says 40% within 60 days and 25% after. Ask which applies when you email them your order number and photos.

Discount codes and sales

We don’t have a code. Their site has men’s and women’s sale sections; on Oct 23, 2026 some BLOX jerseys were $80–$92 and two Blox bibs were $110. Their newsletter signup is on the home page.

Where to ride in it

Their own two rides, both fast, both on the road: Heavy Saturday from Arcadia and Heavy Thursday from Sunnyslope. The rest is on the Heavy Pedal page.

Questions

What is the Heavy Pedal $100 kit?

The $100 Collection is their Attack line: jerseys at $40–$50 and bibs at $50, sold separately, so a jersey and bib come to about $100.

Does Heavy Pedal kit run small?

Their product pages call it a slim race cut. The Attack jersey page says to size up for a looser fit, and the Blox bib page says it runs on the slimmer side.

What is Heavy Pedal’s crash replacement?

A discount on a new garment after a crash. Their warranty page lists 40% off in the first 6 months, 25% after that and 15% after 2 years; a note elsewhere on the site says 40% within 60 days and 25% after. Email sales@theheavypedal.com with your order number and photos.

How long does a Heavy Pedal custom kit take?

About six weeks from an approved design to delivery, with a minimum of 10 pieces.
