Heavy Pedal Phoenix: the rides, the shop, the kit

The Heavy Pedal is a cycling-apparel brand in Phoenix. It sells its own jerseys, bibs and custom team kit from a shop at 4700 N Central Ave, and it runs two road rides through its Strava club, Heavy Pedal Community Rides: Heavy Saturday, every other Saturday morning from Arcadia, and Heavy Thursday, every other Thursday night from Sunnyslope.

We’re not connected to The Heavy Pedal, and nobody paid for this page. It comes from their own site, Strava club and Instagram, checked Oct 23, 2026.

Heavy Pedal Phoenix group rides

Two rides, both on the road, both fast. They’re posted as events in the Heavy Pedal Community Rides club on Strava (1,295 members on Oct 23, 2026). Join the club to see the week’s route and RSVP. Each ride page here has the next date, the start on a map and a calendar file.

Heavy Saturday

The morning one. The club describes a fast 30- to 45-mile loop with a few climbs, starting at Arcadia Dr and the canal, just north of Indian School Rd. Start times move through the year, and the club asks you to be there 15 minutes early because it rolls on time.

Heavy Thursday

The night one. It rolls at 7:00 pm from OHSO + Little O’s on Central Ave in Sunnyslope. All summer the posts said the Piestewa route, up toward the Phoenix Mountain Preserve. The host’s own ride on Oct 22, 2026 was about 20 miles with about 900 feet of climbing.

The Heavy Pedal shop in Phoenix

The shop is at 4700 N. Central Ave. #116, Phoenix, AZ 85012, on Central between Indian School and Camelback. Their Instagram lists the hours as Monday to Friday 11 am–6 pm and Saturday 11 am–4 pm. It’s also where online returns go, so it’s the place to see sizes in person before you order. Email: sales@theheavypedal.com.

What The Heavy Pedal makes

Road and gravel kit: short- and long-sleeve jerseys, bibs and bib tights, a skinsuit, a thermal jacket, base layers and socks, plus custom kit for teams and clubs. Most jerseys run $120–$135 and most bibs $145–$165. The $100 Collection pairs a $40–$50 jersey with a $50 bib. Their product pages describe a race cut that runs slim.

The rides they’ve put on

  • Tour de Presta, Tucson. Sunday, March 15, 2026: a 9 am rollout from Presta Coffee at 4700 E Sunrise Dr, A and B pace, about 30 miles of punchy climbs, with a big crew down from Phoenix.
  • Heavy Quads. A Saturday ride on the club for April 4, 2026, 6:00 am from N Arcadia Dr. It also ran in 2024.
  • Heavy Lemmon Service. A Tucson trip with Saturday Morning Service in spring 2025, $25.

None of these has a 2027 date yet. They get posted on the Strava club and on @heavypedal_phx first.

A little history

The brand says it started as a small bike blog about track bikes. Around 2013 it made the Zephyr, an aluminum track frameset, and The Radavist covered it and the kits that followed. Phoenix New Times once called it a bike crew turned clothing company. Today the kit ships worldwide, and the main Instagram is @theheavypedal.

Questions

Where is The Heavy Pedal in Phoenix?

4700 N. Central Ave. #116, Phoenix, AZ 85012. Their Instagram lists the shop hours as Monday to Friday 11 am to 6 pm and Saturday 11 am to 4 pm.

What is Heavy Thursday?

The Heavy Pedal’s evening road ride: every other Thursday, 7:00 pm rollout from OHSO + Little O’s at 8525 N Central Ave in Sunnyslope. The Oct 22, 2026 ride was about 20 miles with about 900 feet of climbing.

What is Heavy Saturday?

The Heavy Pedal’s morning road ride: every other Saturday, 7:00 am from Arcadia Dr at the canal, just north of Indian School Rd. The host calls it a fast 30- to 45-mile loop with a few climbs.

Are the Heavy Pedal rides no-drop?

Neither ride says no-drop, and the host calls both fast. Join the Heavy Pedal Community Rides club on Strava to see the route before you go.

Does The Heavy Pedal make custom team kits?

Yes. Their custom program starts at 10 pieces and quotes about six weeks from an approved design to delivery. Bring a design or they will draw one.

Is there a Heavy Pedal discount code?

Not from us. Their site has men’s and women’s sale sections, and US orders over $60 ship free.

Is The Heavy Pedal the same as heavypedal.nl?

No. heavypedal.nl is a Dutch builder of cargo vending bikes. The Heavy Pedal is the cycling-apparel brand in Phoenix.
