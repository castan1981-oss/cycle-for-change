For someone I love

  • You can call 988 for them. The 988 Lifeline talks with people who are worried about someone else, not only people in crisis themselves.
  • Families and friends get support too. PFLAG has local chapters and meetings for parents, families and allies of LGBTQ+ people.
  • For a young person in your life, the Trevor Project is there 24/7, and they can reach it themselves.
  • If someone is in danger right now, call 911.

Why this page is here

Riding is part of what kept me steady before I had the words for anything. Cycle for Change rides for the kinds of orgs on this page: in 2027 I ride 10,000 miles, and the money goes through those orgs’ own rides. That’s the connection, and it’s the only ask here. The orgs I ride for

This page points you to credible outside help. It isn't medical advice, and it isn't a substitute for a professional. The phone numbers and directories belong to the organizations named — reach them directly.
