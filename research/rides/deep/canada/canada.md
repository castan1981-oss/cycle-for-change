# Canada deep sweep — area `canada`

Agent: ride scout. Date: Oct 1, 2026. About 160 page fetches. WebSearch was out, and the search engines that answer to curl returned nothing useful for ride queries, so rides came from Meetup (find pages and .ics feeds), provincial federation club directories, club and shop sites, and a public club calendar feed. Bot walls were left alone.

Honest headline: 7 new rides, not 30. Most Canadian clubs are members-only for insurance (Ontario Cycling / Cycling BC / Bicycle Nova Scotia cover), post routes in Facebook groups, Discord, Cyql or Spond, and show no dated schedule to a logged-out reader. Strava club events and shop ride pages were thin to find without search. Montréal, Québec City, Victoria, Edmonton, Winnipeg and Kelowna came up empty. Toronto only has the 6 rides already on the site. The "Couldn't confirm" list below is the way in for a local rider.

## Why these

- **Ottawa Bicycle Club, Sunday Ride** — the club's main weekly road ride, groups from 60 to 129 km, no-drop, with a $10 day pass for a trial. The club's .ics feed re-checks it.
- **Ottawa Bicycle Club, Saturday Gravel Ride** — weekly gravel, strict no-drop, written for people new to gravel. Same feed.
- **One Cycling (Ottawa), Saturday Ride** — open Meetup group, most Saturdays, English and French, coffee or ice cream stop. Next ride Oct 3 is an 8:00 am Gatineau Park loop.
- **Slow and Steady Hikers, Friday Evening Bike Ride (Calgary)** — every Friday 6:15 pm from Heritage Park, 15 km around Glenmore Reservoir. Easy, open, Meetup shows five dated Fridays.
- **Out-n-About Cycling in Metro Vancouver, Weekday Rides** — three guided rides a week, 10:00 am, different start each time, guests welcome, Meetup feed with ten upcoming dates.
- **Saskatoon Cycling Club, Frosty Ramble** — Saturday 1:00 pm winter fat bike rides, November to March, with Horizon 100. On break; resumes in November.
- **Cyclesmith, Thursday Road Ride (Halifax)** — shop ride, Thursday 6:15 pm from the Agricola Street store, 25 to 28 km/h. Medium: the page's latest ride is May 21.

## Where rides are posted here

- **Ottawa Bicycle Club** — `https://obcrides.ca/events` and the feed `https://obcrides.ca/events.ics` (UTC times, every ride since 2020). Machine-readable. Each ride page shows start, groups and registration.
- **Meetup .ics feeds** work for open groups (`/<group>/events/ical/`). Several groups returned "Invalid feed signature" (Victoria E-Bike Club, Montreal Cyclists and Bike Enthusiasts, Ottawa's Lesbian Outdoor Group, MOCWA) and can't be read this way.
- **Meetup find page** (`https://www.meetup.com/find/?keywords=cycling&location=ca--on--Toronto&source=GROUPS`) renders a group list a robot can read. Canadian bike groups on Meetup are few and mostly post one-off rides.
- **Ontario Cycling club directory** `https://ontariocycling.org/club-team-directory/` (a lead list: each club's spotlight page links its site). **Cycling BC affiliates** `https://cyclingbc.net/clubs/affiliates/`. **Cycling Nova Scotia clubs** `https://cyclingns.ca/nova-scotia-cycling-clubs/`. Leads only.
- **Strava** club pages load by vanity name (e.g. `strava.com/clubs/OWCC`) but show no events; a single event URL shows the next date, and none turned up for these cities.
- **Members-only apps** the clubs use: Cyql (Ottawa Women's Cycling Club), Discord (Beaches Cycling Club), Spond (Etobicoke, Bespoke), Facebook groups (Spoke Sisters, City of Lakes), CCN Bikes for memberships and Try-it / day passes (`ccnbikes.com`).
- **Wild Apricot** (Toronto Bicycling Network) lists the next 20 events at `https://www.tbn.ca/Next20events`. Its other open weekday rides (Tuesday Ravine, Wednesday Wheelies, Sunday Urban Roller, Sunday Tourist, Country Cruises, No-Drop Country Rides) are host-cap extras, see below.
- **Cloudflare / bot walls, not touched:** cycleto.ca (403), massecritique.ca (429), cyclonordsud.org (202), revolutioncycle.ca (403), rockylakecycling.ca inner pages (429).
