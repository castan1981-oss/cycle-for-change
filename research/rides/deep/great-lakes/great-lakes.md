## Where rides are posted here

- **ClubExpress "future events" lists.** The best source in the area. URL pattern: `<club site>/content.aspx?page_id=4001&club_id=<id>&action=cira&vm=Future&sif=0`. Worked for Cincinnati Cycle Club (772752), Dayton Cycling Club (455464), NMMBA (533979), TCBA (604795), Blackhawk BSC (320178), and Akron Bicycle Club on its own domain without a club_id. Each event lists date, time, start and pace.
- **Public Google Calendar ICS.** Springfield Bicycle Club, Detroit Greenways Coalition community calendar, Cleveland Touring Club (11 calendars). Plain text, dated, easy to re-check. The Detroit one is secondary: the coalition says it doesn't host the rides, it lists them.
- **Queen City Bike calendar** (Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky). Times in the page are UTC. RSS at `/feed.xml`.
- **Outdoor-Pursuits** (Columbus area) event pages.
- **Club ride pages with no dates** (Prairie Cycle Club, Illinois Valley Wheelm'n, Ridenfaden, 3RVS). Fine for medium. Not proof of life by themselves.
- **Facebook and Instagram.** Most Detroit, Indianapolis, Columbus and Ann Arbor community rides live here and nowhere else. See Couldn't confirm.
- **Bot walls.** centralincycling.org (HTTP 202 challenge, Indianapolis) and mcleancountywheelers.com (307 redirect challenge, Normal IL). Not worked around, per the brief.
- **Shop pages** are often undated or still say "2025". I only confirmed one (Pedal Power, Lexington) because the same page carried July 2026 rides on matching weekdays.
