Strava club event pages could not be searched (no search engine worked; Bing and DuckDuckGo returned junk or a captcha), Meetup has almost nothing for cycling in Mexico, and the two Meetup groups found (Brompton México, GTO Mountain Bikers) have empty calendars. What did load: shop sites with their own calendars (Ciclópolis), municipal government pages, and the Mexico City police agenda of escorted rides, which La Silla Rota republishes every weekday morning. That agenda is how the three collective rides were found. Leads that failed the proof test are listed below for a local rider to check.

## Where rides are posted here

- **Mexico City police ride agenda.** The Secretaría de Seguridad Ciudadana lists every escorted ride (name, time, start, destination, group size) each weekday. La Silla Rota republishes it as "Marchas, concentraciones y cierres viales hoy ... en CDMX", with a "Rodadas ciclistas" block: topic page https://lasillarota.com/temas/movilizaciones-cdmx-48751.html. This is the best robot-readable proof of life for CDMX collectives, but it is a secondary source and lists only rides that filed. Excélsior, Sin Embargo, 24 Horas and UnoTV republish it too.
- **Shop calendars.** Ciclópolis https://www.ciclopolis.mx/pages/eventos (Shopify page, two months of dated rides). Instagram @ciclopolismx.
- **City government news pages.** saltillo.gob.mx, www2.toluca.gob.mx, guadalajara.gob.mx, municipiodequeretaro.gob.mx. Local papers repeat each announcement (El Informador, Vanguardia, El Sol de México regional editions).
- **Google News RSS** (`news.google.com/rss/search?q=...`) works from a shell and is the one way to find local announcements by name. The links it returns are redirect-wrapped and need decoding to open.
- **Meetup** (`/find/?keywords=cycling&location=mx--...`) renders server-side and lists groups, but there are very few cycling groups in Mexico.
- **Not readable:** Strava, Instagram, Facebook (all collectives), viarecreactiva.org and merida.gob.mx (403 / bot wall), sipse.com, cambiodemichoacan.com.mx, metapolitica.news (Cloudflare "Just a moment").
- Words to search: rodada, rodada nocturna, paseo dominical, vía recreativa, ciclovía recreativa, ruta recreativa, masa crítica, biciescuela.
