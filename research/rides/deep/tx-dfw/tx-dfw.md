## Why these

- **Tuesday Lake Laps (Trek Dallas Park Cities).** The beginner-oriented lake ride from Snider Plaza, 15 to 20 miles at 13 to 16 mph, free, with a listed session tonight's week.
- **Thursday Lake Laps (Trek Dallas Park Cities).** The fast one from the same shop: 25 to 35 miles at 20 mph or better.
- **The Saturday Ride, Trek Fort Worth South.** Intermediate Saturday road ride, about 28 miles at 15 to 17 mph; a drop ride, so it says so.
- **Ride Club Thursday Night Gravel Ride (Fort Worth).** Gravel on a Thursday night with two pace groups and a sweep, so no-drop, then beer on a patio. The only gravel ride with a live date in Fort Worth.
- **Ride Club Monthly Sunday Road Ride (Flower Mound).** A last-Sunday road ride with a new route each month, 30 to 40 miles at 16 to 17 mph.
- **GDB Mesquite Casual.** A no-drop Thursday evening ride from Poteet High School in Mesquite, 30 to 32 miles at 14 to 16 mph. East side of the metro, where little is listed.
- **GDB Downtown Gelato Ride.** Evening loop through Park Cities, Turtle Creek, Deep Ellum and Swiss Avenue with a gelato stop. No drops.
- **GDB Sunday Roll.** A Sunday "Explore Dallas" ride with a new route each week, 13 to 14 mph. The host says not for beginners.
- **Taco Tuesday Social Ride (Allen).** The weekly trail ride from Velvet Taco: about 12 paved miles at a social pace, then food.
- **A Couple Laps Around White Rock.** The Cycling Trail Riders' monthly new-member ride, no-drop, split into two or three pace groups.
- **Community Beer Co. Cycling Group Ride.** A free ride every other weekend morning from the brewery, groups for every rider, with a Halloween costume ride on Oct 31.
- **Tuesday Back to Basics (Dallas Pedals & Pints).** Slower weekly ride for new riders and people coming back to the bike, from White Rock Alehouse.
- **Eastside Taco Ride.** A no-drop, family-, dog- and e-bike-friendly monthly ride to a different taco stop; the host calls it a good first group ride.
- **The Meteor Women's Ride.** A monthly women's ride from The Meteor Cafe, 15 miles or less at 12 to 14 mph, with pizza after and the 2026 dates posted.
- **DBC Second Saturday Coffee Social and Ride.** The Dallas Bicycle Coalition's own monthly coffee social, then a 10 mph hour-long ride. Good way to meet the local advocates.
- **White Rock Ramble.** A last-Saturday mixed-surface loop (road, gravel, singletrack) around the lake; the only gravel-leaning monthly ride I found in Dallas.
- **Wednesday Night Trail Party (Northshore).** Weekly DORBA mountain bike ride on the big Lake Grapevine trail system.
- **Thursday Group Ride (Oak Cliff Nature Preserve).** DORBA's Thursday MTB ride in Oak Cliff.
- **Wednesday Group Ride (Frisco NW Community Park).** DORBA's Wednesday MTB ride in Frisco.
- **Anna May Daulton Joe Pool Lake, Rusty Chain Gang (Grand Prairie).** An FWBA Saturday ride with an always-offered slow 16-mile group at 12 to 14 mph and a stated no-drop policy.
- **Rusty Romp (Benbrook).** FWBA weekday-morning rides from the Benbrook YMCA, Tuesday and Thursday at 8:00 am, with a slow 16 to 20 mile group. Park entry is $2 by card.

## Where rides are posted here

DFW is a Facebook-and-Instagram town. The best machine-readable places, in order:

1. **Meetup ICS feeds.** `meetup.com/<group>/events/ical/` for: `greater-dallas-bicyclists`, `dallas-cycling-trail-riders-and-weekend-pub-crawlers`, `dallas-pedals-and-pints`, `community-beer-co-cycling-group`. All four list dated events. Meetup topic pages gave these names; I found no other cycling Meetup group with real rides.
2. **Eventbrite organizer pages for Trek stores.** Each Trek shop posts its group-ride series as a multi-date event, and the event page shows `nextAvailableSession` and the series start and end dates: Trek Dallas Park Cities (organizer 46367112593), Trek Fort Worth South (49087200853), Trek Flower Mound (49086747703). Trek Allen's road rides ended Oct 2025 and Trek Arlington South's club ride ended Jan 2025.
3. **BikeDFW community calendar.** Tockify feed `https://tockify.com/api/feeds/ics/commevents` (page: `bikedfw.org/community-calendar.html`). Region-wide, submitted by hosts.
4. **Dallas Bicycle Coalition social rides page.** `dallasbicyclecoalition.org/social-rides/`. The events sit in the page's own data (a Sanity export inside an Astro island), so a plain fetch of the text shows nothing. Entries carry a created date but no edit date.
5. **Club calendars.** FWBA's home page lists the next seven rides with dates (`fwbaclub.org`, Wild Apricot; each ride has an `event-<id>` page). PBA (`planobicycle.org`) and Lone Star Cyclists use ClubExpress calendars that need JavaScript. DORBA's weekly rides page (`dorba.org`, module 647759) lists every weekly trail ride with no dates.
6. **Bike Mart's group rides page** (`bikemart.com/pages/group-rides`) is an Elfsight events widget. Its settings load from `core.service.elfsight.com/p/boot/` and hold every weekly series.
7. **Strava club pages** show "Upcoming Club Event" but hide it behind a sign-up (Velo Republic, Ride Around Dallas). Strava event pages I found by search were all out of area or years old.
