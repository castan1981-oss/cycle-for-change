# Shared sites, opened Nov 23, 2026

The Nov 19 audit left the shared sites (a site that carries rides for three or more hosts) waiting for someone to open
them and say which are community calendars like msp.bike. On Nov 23, 2026 every one of the 54 then on the list was
opened (two could not be read, noted below). The line drawn: **a community calendar is a calendar or directory whose
job is listing the place's rides, including rides its keeper doesn't run.** A page on a club's, shop's, city's or
paper's site that happens to mention other people's rides is a roundup: the shared-site rule already stops us calling
it "the host's website", and it gets no row.
