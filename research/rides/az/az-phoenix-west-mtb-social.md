## Why these

1. **Bear Claw Ride, Peoria.** The slowest ride I found. Six miles to a bakery, coffee, four miles back, at 10 to 13 mph. The host calls it strictly social and very casual. Every other Sunday, 6:30 am, from Rio Vista Community Park.
2. **Stadium Ride, Peoria.** The step up from Bear Claw, on the other Sundays. Ten miles out to a Carls Jr breakfast and ten back at 11 to 20+ mph. The host says it is no-drop and means it.
3. **Dirty Gravel/MTB Rides, Surprise.** The one regular off-road ride I could verify in the West Valley. Every other Saturday at 7:00 am, a new trailhead each time, as short as 4 miles. Bring a mountain bike, a gravel bike or a cruiser. Next: Oct 10 from the Sun Valley Trailhead.
4. **Tuesday Casual Ride, Sun City.** The club's casual ride. An average pace, the distance set by who shows up, beginners welcome, no-drop. Weekly, 8:00 am in October.
5. **Thursday Ride, Sun City.** 30 to 50+ miles at about 13 to 15 mph, no-drop. The club calls it more serious than the Tuesday ride.
6. **All Levels: Show & Go, Peoria.** Friday, 7:00 am, from the back lot at SouthWest Bicycles. No fixed route. The group works out where to go. The club says all its rides are no-drop.
7. **Sunday C Ride, Peoria.** 26 to 29 miles at 14 to 16 mph with a rest stop, and coffee after. The start changes every week. Oct 4 leaves from the Panera on Happy Valley Rd.
8. **Saturday Group Ride (A and B), Goodyear.** The West Valley's main Saturday road ride. A 40-mile or 62-mile option from the Red Robin on McDowell Rd at 6:30 am, with optional early miles at 5:30. The club says it welcomes new riders.
9. **Wednesday Happy Ride, Goodyear.** A mid-week ride that stays together and picks a new route every week, with a downwind ride back to the Red Robin. The start time is on Facebook the day before.
10. **Sunday Recovery Ride, Goodyear.** A set 35-mile loop at recovery pace with a refuel stop in Verrado. Riders who want to push the second half are let go.
11. **Silent Sunday at South Mountain, Phoenix.** The park's main road closes to cars every Sunday: 5 am to 7 pm on the 4th Sunday, 5 am to 10 am on the others. A car-free climb to ride on your own or with friends.

**Rhythm.** Early starts and short days move everything.
- Sun City Cycling Club's Tuesday and Thursday rides start at 5:30 am in July, 6:00 in June and August, 7:00 in May and September, 8:00 in April, October and November, and 9:00 from December to March (March is 9:00, then 8:00).

- West Valley Casual Bike Rides will not ride in dust storms, rain, high winds, lightning or extreme heat. Its beginner ride cancels above 105 degrees. Check the event page an hour before. Four of its rides were cancelled between Sept 23 and 30.
- Cooler weather moves the Saturday long rides later (the Long Ride text says 8 am "for now").

**Visitor norms.**
- West Valley Casual Bike Rides: a helmet is required and the group won't let you ride without one. Sign the waiver (a link on every event), RSVP on Meetup, and un-RSVP if you can't come, because a leader waits for anyone signed up. Lights, flat-fix gear and plenty of water. No earbuds.
- Sun City Cycling Club: guests are welcome. The club's insurance covers a guest on the first ride only, then it asks you to join. Everyone signs that day's waiver. A certified helmet is required and lights are strongly encouraged. No Class 2 or throttle e-bikes. Rain at the start cancels the ride, and the club posts cancellations on its home page.
- SouthWest Bicycles: a first-time guest can ride once and is covered, then you must join. Non-members sign a waiver every time. A helmet is required. Leaders ask you to arrive early and roll on time. Leaders post each week's start by midweek, and it can change.
- West Valley Cycle: no permits, no insurance, no dues, ride at your own risk. Its core values include welcoming new riders. Start times are on its Facebook group, so ask the club contact how to get in.
- South Mountain: Silent Sunday is a closed road, not a led ride. Drivers park at the Activity Complex. City staff and emergency vehicles may be on the road at any time.
- Bring lights for any start before sunrise.
