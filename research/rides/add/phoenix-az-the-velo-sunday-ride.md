- **The Velo Sunday ride.** What's known: a Phoenix New Times piece dated Jul 14, 2015 says the shop ran "a weekly group ride on Sundays at 6 a.m.", up to 40 riders, from the shop to the South Mountain summit and back, with coffee after — at the old 828 N 2nd St location. A TrainerRoad forum post dated May 20, 2019 says "Velo Bikes Sunday ride from their shop downtown PHX. Check at the shop for times. Usually they ride up SOMO and back but once a month they do the pistol loop." Both are over 12 months old, third-party, and predate the move to 7th Street. The shop's current site lists only the Wednesday ride. No Strava event page for any Velo ride turned up in search; the club's event list needs a login.
