# Friday Shop Ride, Bicycle Haüs (Scottsdale)

Robert's own tip, Oct 8, 2026. He rides it every Friday from his home shop.

- Every Friday, 7:00 am, from Bicycle Haüs, 7113 E 1st Ave, Scottsdale.
- Loops around Paradise Valley and finishes at La Grande Orange (4410 N 40th St, Phoenix). Everyone rides home from there.
- About 20 miles. Social, no-drop.
