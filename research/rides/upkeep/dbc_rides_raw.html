Dallas has an amazing bike community with tons of bike rides at all levels for you to explore our city on two wheels, make new friends, and meet other bike advocates.

Weekly, monthly, and some pop up rides are listed. Confirm the ride details with the host organization before you attend. Dates and times frequently change. Don't forget to check the DBC main calendar for other events!

If you notice any changes that need to be made, please email contact@dallasbicyclecoalition.org or dm us on instagram or facebook.
