# Bike brands — who makes what, who owns whom

Evergreen reference, accurate to mid-2026 unless marked. Ownership changes fast in
this industry; anything tagged **(verify)** should be checked before it goes in
public copy. 2025–26 news is in [06-current-events-2026.md](06-current-events-2026.md).

## 0. How the industry actually works

- **Almost every bike is made in Taiwan, China, Vietnam or Cambodia**, whatever
  the headbadge says. Giant and Merida (Taiwan) are the two giant OEMs and also
  sell under their own names. Other big contract makers: Ideal, Fairly, Hodaka,
  Astro, Kinesis (Taiwan); Fritz Jou, Xidesheng/XDS (China); A-Pro. Carbon
  frames mostly come from a handful of Chinese and Taiwanese plants.
- **"Brand" means design, spec, marketing, warranty and dealer network.** A few
  do real domestic manufacturing: Trek's Waterloo carbon (small volume),
  Allied (Arkansas), Moots (Colorado), Pinarello and Colnago top frames (Italy;
  most models are Asian), Look (Nevers), Time (Slovakia/France), Bianchi's
  Treviglio plant (2024–), Brompton (London), Pashley (Stratford), Hope
  (Lancashire), Chris King, Paul, White Industries, Phil Wood (US parts).
- **Three drivetrain makers matter**: Shimano (Japan, ~60–70% of the market),
  SRAM (Chicago, private), Campagnolo (Vicenza, family). Everything else is
  niche or OEM-only (microSHIFT, LTWOO, Sensah, Wheeltop).
- **Distribution in the US**: brands sell through independent bike dealers
  (IBDs), brand-owned stores (Trek and Specialized own hundreds of shops),
  REI, big-box (Walmart/Target = Pacific Cycle/Huffy/Kent), and direct to
  consumer (Canyon, Ribble, Fezzari→Ari, State, Aventon, Lectric, Rad). QBP
  (Quality Bicycle Products, Minnesota) is the dominant US parts distributor
  and owns Salsa, Surly, 45NRTH, Whisky, Teravail, All-City (discontinued 2024).
- **Two Dutch conglomerates own a startling share of brands**: Pon Holdings
  and Accell Group (below).

## 2. The Dutch conglomerates

**Pon Holdings** (Almere; also VW importer, Caterpillar dealer): Gazelle,
Cervélo, Santa Cruz, Juliana, Cannondale, GT (paused 2025, verify), Schwinn,
Mongoose, Caloi (Brazil), Focus, Kalkhoff, Union, Urban Arrow, Swapfiets
(subscription), Mike's Bikes (US retail). Bought Dorel Sports for ~$810M in
2021, making Pon the world's largest bike company by revenue.

**Accell Group** (Heerenveen; taken private by KKR 2022; debt restructured
2024; handed to lenders Feb 2026; **suspension of payments / insolvency
granted 6 Aug 2026**, brands may be sold off individually): Raleigh (UK),
Haibike, Ghost, Lapierre (separate French proceedings), Batavus, Koga,
Sparta, Winora, Babboe (cargo; 2024 safety recall crisis), XLC parts.

## 3. Components and parts

### Drivetrains and brakes
- **Shimano** (Sakai, Osaka; 1921; also fishing tackle). Road: Dura-Ace
  (R9200 12-speed Di2), Ultegra, 105 (Di2 and mechanical 12-speed), Tiagra,
  Sora, Claris. Gravel: GRX (12-speed Di2 and mechanical). MTB: XTR (new
  M9200 Di2 wireless 2025), XT, SLX, Deore, Cues (2023, replaces the low-end
  road/MTB mess). E-bike: EP8/EP801, EP6, STEPS. Also **Pearl Izumi**, **Lazer**
  helmets, PRO components. Fought the SRAM AXS patent war and a 2021–23
  crank-failure recall (Hollowtech II, ~2.8M cranks).
- **SRAM** (Chicago; 1987; private; founders Stan Day et al.). Road: Red AXS
  (2024 redesign), Force, Rival, Apex; XPLR gravel; MTB: XX/X0/GX/S1000
  Eagle Transmission (direct-mount, 2023), Maven brakes (2024), Motive.
  Owns **RockShox**, **Zipp**, **Quarq**, **Truvativ**, **Avid**, **Time
  pedals** (2021), **Hammerhead** (Karoo computers, 2022). Sponsors
  Lidl–Trek, Movistar, Canyon//SRAM (women), many MTB teams.
- **Campagnolo** (Vicenza; 1933; Valentino Campagnolo; minority stake sold
  Sep 2026 to Ivan Glasenberg's SPAC SA, Pinarello's owner, family keeps
  control). Super Record 13 (wireless), Record 13 (2026), Chorus, Ekar
  (13-speed gravel, 2020), **Fulcrum** wheels. Small pro presence (Cofidis,
  Pinarello–Q36.5). Romance brand.
- **Classified** (Belgium): Powershift hub, 2-speed internal in the rear hub,
  replaces a front derailleur. **Pinion** (Germany): gearbox, plus MGU e-bike
  motor-gearbox (2023). **Rohloff**: 14-speed hub. **Gates**: carbon belt.
  **Sturmey-Archer** (now Sun Race, Taiwan). **Enviolo**: CVT hub.
- Chinese groupsets rising: **LTWOO** (eRX/eGR wireless), **Wheeltop** (EDS),
  **Sensah**. Real, cheap, improving; not yet pro-level.

## 8. Brand facts people get wrong

- Giant makes bikes for other brands, but Trek and Specialized carbon comes
  mostly from other Taiwanese/Chinese factories, not Giant.
- Cannondale is Dutch-owned (Pon) and Taiwan-made; the Pennsylvania era
  ended in 2009.
- Colnago is Emirati-owned; Pinarello is owned by a former Glencore CEO;
  Rapha is owned by the Walton (Walmart) heirs.
- Schwinn the company died in 1992; the name is a Pon/Pacific Cycle label.
- "SRAM" is not an acronym for anything sensible (it's from founder names:
  Scott, Ray, Sam).
- Shimano is the biggest bike company you can't buy a bike from.
- Specialized's biggest shareholder is Merida, a Taiwanese OEM.
