## 1. What happened in the 2026 pro season

### Grand Tours
- **Giro d'Italia (8–31 May, Nesebar, Bulgaria → Rome).** Jonas Vingegaard
  (Visma–Lease a Bike) won on his Giro debut, five stage wins, becoming the
  eighth man to win all three Grand Tours. Podium: Felix Gall +5:22, Jai
  Hindley +6:25. Points Paul Magnier, mountains Giulio Ciccone, young rider
  Afonso Eulálio. Pogačar and Evenepoel both skipped it. [C]
  (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Giro_d'Italia;
  https://inrng.com/2026/06/giro-2026-review/)
- **Tour de France (4–26 July, Barcelona → Paris).** Tadej Pogačar won his
  **fifth Tour**, equalling Anquetil, Merckx, Hinault and Induráin, at a
  record 43.2 km/h average. Podium: Remco Evenepoel +6:26 (two stage wins,
  first year at Red Bull–Bora), Isaac del Toro +9:42 (white jersey). Green
  Mads Pedersen, polka dot Richard Carapaz. Opened with a team time trial in
  Barcelona; two Alpe d'Huez finishes; Pogačar broke Pantani's Alpe record.
  Vingegaard crashed out on stage 15 with a broken collarbone and did not
  race again in 2026. Stage 21 was shortened because security staff were
  sent to Gironde wildfires. [C]
  (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Tour_de_France;
  https://www.cyclingnews.com/pro-cycling/racing/jonas-vingegaard-out-for-rest-of-season-with-no-return-possible-after-crash/)
- **Tour de France Femmes (1–9 Aug, Lausanne → Nice).** Demi Vollering
  (FDJ United–SUEZ) won her second, soloing on the Col d'Èze finale. Podium:
  Kasia Niewiadoma-Phinney +1:18, Elisa Longo Borghini +4:29. Points Lorena
  Wiebes, mountains Puck Pieterse. Defending champion Pauline Ferrand-Prévot
  abandoned ill before stage 8. [C]
  (https://www.cyclingweekly.com/racing/reigning-champion-pauline-ferrand-prevot-pulls-out-of-tour-de-france-femmes-ahead-of-stage-8)
- **Vuelta a España (22 Aug – 13 Sep, Monaco → Granada).** Enric Mas
  (Movistar) won his first Grand Tour, the first Spanish Vuelta winner since
  Contador in 2014. Podium: Primož Roglič +2:15, Felix Gall +2:44. Wout van
  Aert took green and super-combativity with two stage wins; Matthew Brennan
  (Visma) won five sprints. **Pogačar crashed on stage 8** while leading by
  3:50: concussion, broken clavicle, stable C7 fracture, surgery, season
  over, first Grand Tour DNF of his career. Stage 3 was cancelled mid-race
  in a hailstorm. [C]
  (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Vuelta_a_España;
  https://www.cyclingnews.com/pro-cycling/teams-riders/tadej-pogacar-abandons-the-vuelta-a-espana-after-crash-on-stage-8/)

## 2. Industry and brands, 2026

### Health of the business
- Still working through the post-COVID glut. Taiwan's June 2026 numbers were
  read as the first sign of recovery (Giant June +16% y/y) but H1 2026 Giant
  was still −10.5% and Merida −15.3%. Giant's January was −22%. [C]
  (https://www.bicycleretailer.com/international/2026/07/10/giants-june-revenues-16-while-merida-and-ideal-revenues-are-down)
- **Accell Group** (Haibike, Ghost, Lapierre, Winora, Raleigh UK, Batavus,
  Koga, Babboe) is the year's big failure: KKR handed it to lenders in Feb;
  a takeover by Singapore's Dutech collapsed; **suspension of payments
  (insolvency) granted 6 Aug 2026**; Lapierre in separate French proceedings;
  brands may be sold individually. [C]
  (https://bikebiz.com/accell-group-files-for-insolvency-as-takeover-negotiations-collapse/)
- **Trek**: third round of layoffs in mid-January 2026 after ~15% cuts in
  Sept 2025; reporting points to overstock, debt service and weak
  Trek-owned retail. CPSC recall of ~19,900 FX+ 1 / Electra Townie Go! for
  rear-wheel bolts (Jan). [C]
  (https://escapecollective.com/layoffs-overstock-retail-decline-and-debt-trek-is-in-trouble/)

- **Shimano**: FY2025 bike +3%; Q1 2026 operating income −46%; H1 sales flat,

  all components from Aug 2026**; **$11.5M CPSC penalty** (Mar) for late

## 3. Advocacy, infrastructure, culture, 2026

### US federal
- Surface transportation law expires **30 Sep 2026**. The House committee
  passed the **BUILD America 250 Act** (H.R. 8870) 62–2 on 22 May: ~$580bn,
  five years, keeps Transportation Alternatives (~$1.5bn/yr, up to +10%) and
  Safe Streets and Roads for All, allows e-bikes on federally funded trails
  subject to local approval. No floor vote; the Senate passed a short-term
  extension to 11 Dec on 8 Aug. **Unresolved as of mid-September.** [C]
  (https://bikeleague.org/our-first-look-at-transportation-reauthorization/)
- USDOT Secretary Duffy redirected ~$1.73bn of grants away from what he
  called "DEI bike lanes" (July), rescinded active-transportation grants
  (San Diego County, Fairfield AL, Boston Mattapan), proposed ending the
  ATIIP trail program, urged senators to limit bike-lane funding and remove
  lanes that "contribute to congestion," and dropped bike lanes and speed
  cameras from USDOT's safety-countermeasure list. [C]
  (https://www.npr.org/2026/08/15/nx-s1-5900915/transportation-reporter-on-trump-administrations-efforts-against-dei-bike-lanes)

### Safety numbers
- NHTSA final 2024: **1,103 cyclists killed**, down 6% from 2023's record;
  81% urban, 30% at intersections, 87% male, average age 48. 2025 early
  estimate: cyclist deaths up ~4% while overall traffic deaths fell. [C]
  (https://crashstats.nhtsa.dot.gov/Api/Public/ViewPublication/813822)
- Netherlands 2025: 281 cyclists killed (+35%, highest since 2007), ~40%
  involving e-bikes. [C]
- Hoboken passed eight years with zero traffic deaths; Portland's 39 deaths
  in 2025 were its lowest since 2018. [C]

### Mental-health research, 2024–26 (cite these, with their limits)
- **UK Biobank cohort, 240,547 workers** (Translational Psychiatry, Feb
  2025): cycle commuting HR 0.78 for depression (95% CI 0.67–0.89) and 0.78
  for anxiety (0.68–0.90); inflammation mediated ~19%. Observational.
  (https://www.nature.com/articles/s41398-024-03219-w)
- **Scoping review of 87 bicycling-intervention studies** (Frontiers in
  Sports and Active Living, 2026): social-wellbeing benefits in every study
  that measured it; positive psychological effects in 67%; multi-week
  programs showed the largest depressive-symptom drops. Not pooled.
  (https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/sports-and-active-living/articles/10.3389/fspor.2026.1807791/full)
- **Noetel et al., BMJ 2024** network meta-analysis (218 studies, 14,170
  participants): walking/jogging g −0.62, yoga −0.55, strength −0.49, mixed
  aerobic −0.43 vs active controls; benefits scale with intensity; certainty
  rated low. (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38355154/)
- **Exercise and substance use disorder** meta-analysis (Experimental and
  Clinical Psychopharmacology, June 2025; 17 studies, 1,363 participants):
  d −0.58 on substance-use outcomes, craving −0.86; blinding and
  heterogeneity flagged. WHO 2024 guidelines list exercise as an adjunct.
  (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12619126/)
- **Trevor Project 2025 survey** (published 6 May 2026; 16,667 youth 13–24):
  36% seriously considered suicide (40% trans/nonbinary), ~1 in 10 attempted,
  62% anxiety, 47% depression, 44% who wanted care could not get it.
  (https://www.thetrevorproject.org/survey-2025/)
- Programs: Outride's Riding for Focus (225+ schools; +9% wellbeing for
  girls), Cycling UK's Cycle for Health (1,000+ reached; self-reported),
  Cycling Minds (Northumberland e-bike rides), Bike Minded (Bristol).
