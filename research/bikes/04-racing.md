# Racing — how the sport is structured, who matters, what the words mean

Evergreen reference, updated through the 2026 season. Season-specific results
and news are in [06-current-events-2026.md](06-current-events-2026.md).

## 1. Governance and calendar

- **UCI** (Union Cycliste Internationale, Aigle, Switzerland; president David
  Lappartient since 2017). Sets rules, runs World Championships, sanctions
  races. Anti-doping is run by the **ITA** (International Testing Agency).
  **SafeR** is the rider-safety body created 2023–24 (yellow cards, GPS
  tracking, barrier standards).
- **Men's road tiers**: **WorldTour** (18 teams, licensed on three-year
  cycles, must ride all WorldTour races), **ProTeams** (second division;
  top two get automatic Grand Tour invites), **Continental**. **Women's
  WorldTour** (15 teams in 2026) and ProTeams below.
- **Season shape**: January Australia (Tour Down Under) → February Middle East
  (UAE Tour) and Opening Weekend (Omloop Het Nieuwsblad) → March Strade
  Bianche, Paris–Nice / Tirreno–Adriatico, Milan–San Remo → April cobbled
  classics (E3, Gent–Wevelgem, Dwars, **Flanders**, **Roubaix**) then Ardennes
  (Amstel, Flèche, **Liège**) → May **Giro** → June Dauphiné / Suisse →
  July **Tour** → August Tour de France Femmes, San Sebastián, **Vuelta** →
  September Canada (Québec/Montréal), **Worlds** → October **Lombardia**,
  Paris–Tours, Gravel Worlds → cyclocross winter.
- **Five Monuments**: Milan–San Remo (longest, ~290 km, "the sprinter's
  classic"), Tour of Flanders (Ronde; bergs and cobbles), Paris–Roubaix ("Hell
  of the North," cobbled sectors, velodrome finish), Liège–Bastogne–Liège
  ("La Doyenne," oldest, 1892, hilly), Il Lombardia ("race of the falling
  leaves").
- **Three Grand Tours**: Giro (May, pink jersey / maglia rosa), Tour (July,
  yellow / maillot jaune; green = points, polka dot = mountains, white =
  best young rider), Vuelta (Aug–Sep, red / la roja). Three weeks, 21
  stages, two rest days.
- **Worlds**: rainbow jersey, ridden by nation not trade team, road race and
  ITT for elite, U23, junior, plus mixed relay. Winner wears the rainbow
  jersey for a year in that discipline. Since 2023, "Super Worlds" combine
  all disciplines every four years (Glasgow 2023, Haute-Savoie 2027).
- **Olympics**: road race, ITT, track (sprint, keirin, team sprint, team
  pursuit, omnium, madison), MTB XCO, BMX racing, BMX freestyle. LA 2028
  track at Carson.

## 2. How a road race works (for writing about it)

- **Peloton** = the main bunch. **Breakaway** = riders ahead. **Domestique**
  = helper. **GC** = general classification (overall time). **Lead-out** =
  sprinter's train. **Echelon** = crosswind formation. **Grupetto / autobus**
  = sprinters surviving a mountain stage inside the time cut.
- **Drafting** saves ~30–40% of energy; that is why racing looks like it
  does. Wattages: pro climbing ~6–6.5 W/kg for 30–40 min; sprints 1,500–
  1,900 W; amateur FTP 2.5–3.5 W/kg.
- **Race radio**, **team cars**, **neutral service**, **feed zones**,
  **musette**, **bidon**, **KOM/QOM** (king/queen of the mountains),
  **intermediate sprints**, **time bonuses**, **time cut** (a percentage of
  the winner's time), **commissaires** (officials).
- **Time trial**: "the race of truth," alone against the clock. TTT = team
  time trial. **Prologue** = short opening TT.
- **Hour Record**: farthest distance in one hour on a track. Men: Filippo
  Ganna 56.792 km (2022). Women: Vittoria Bussi 50.267 km (2023) (verify
  latest).

## 5. Doping — how to write about it

- Peak era ~1991–2008 (EPO, blood transfusions, testosterone, HGH). Festina
  1998, Armstrong/US Postal (USADA 2012), Operación Puerto 2006, Landis 2006,
  Rasmussen 2007, Contador clenbuterol 2010, Team Sky "jiffy bag" and
  Freeman tribunal (2021), Froome salbutamol (cleared 2018).
- **Biological passport** (2008) and the ITA taking over (2021) reduced
  systematic doping; the sport's own view is it is cleaner but not clean.
  Motor doping ("technological fraud") is checked with tablets and X-rays;
  Femke Van den Driessche (2016 CX) is the one confirmed case. The 2026 UCI
  "tech fraud" rules add frame-marking checks.
- Modern speeds (2025–26 Tours the fastest ever) are mostly explained by
  bikes, tires, aero, nutrition (120 g carbs/hour), altitude camps, and
  better talent ID. Say that, then note the skepticism honestly.
- Write "stripped" or "vacated," not "won," for Armstrong 1999–2005 and
  Landis 2006.

## 6. Gravel, ultra, and the amateur scene

- **Gravel**: Unbound Gravel (Emporia, Kansas, late May/early June; 200 mi,
  plus XL 350 mi and 100/50/25), Life Time Grand Prix (six-race series with
  a $350k purse), SBT GRVL, Belgian Waffle Ride, Big Sugar, Crusher in the
  Tushar, Mid South, The Rift (Iceland), Traka (Spain), UCI Gravel Worlds
  (since 2022; Nannup, Australia in 2026). Privateer culture, "spirit of
  gravel" vs pros arguing about aero bars and team tactics.
- **Ultra / bikepacking**: Tour Divide (2,700 mi, Banff to Antelope Wells,
  June; record Victor Bosoni 2026, 11d 8h), Transcontinental Race (TCR,
  Europe), Silk Road Mountain Race (Kyrgyzstan), Atlas Mountain Race, Race
  Across America (RAAM, supported), Paris–Brest–Paris (1,200 km, every four
  years; randonneuring / audax culture; next 2027). Lael Wilcox women's
  round-the-world record 108 days (2024); Mark Beaumont men's 78 days (2017).
- **Everesting**: 8,848 m on one climb in one ride (Hells 500 rules).
- **Fondos / charity rides**: Gran Fondo NY, Levi's GranFondo, El Tour de
  Tucson, RAGBRAI (Iowa, since 1973, the original), Pan-Mass Challenge
  (~$78M/yr for Dana-Farber), AIDS/LifeCycle (ended 2025; successors Cycle to
  Zero and Center Ride Out), Ride for Mental Health.
- **Amateur racing**: USA Cycling categories (Cat 5 → 1; masters), crits are
  the American discipline, collegiate cycling, National Cycling League
  (folded 2024), Zwift racing (categories A–D by W/kg), Strava segments as
  the everyday competition.

## 7. Pro cycling business

- Teams are sponsor-funded (no ticket or TV revenue reaches them); budgets
  from ~€10M to €60M+ (UAE, Visma, Ineos, Red Bull). Riders' minimum salary
  ~€45k (WorldTour men); women's WWT minimum ~€38k. Pogačar's contract is
  reported at ~€8M/year.
- Race organizers: **ASO** (Amaury Sport Organisation: Tour, Vuelta,
  Paris–Roubaix, Liège, Dauphiné, Paris–Nice; also the Dakar), **RCS**
  (Gazzetta: Giro, MSR, Lombardia, Strade, Tirreno), **Flanders Classics**
  (Ronde, Gent–Wevelgem, E3, Omloop, Amstel from 2025?). Organizers keep TV
  money; that is the fight behind **One Cycling** (Saudi-backed league
  proposal, rejected by the UCI in June 2026).
- Rider union: **CPA**. Teams' association: **AIGCP**. Points system: UCI
  ranking decides relegation from the WorldTour at the end of each 3-year
  cycle, which is why teams chase points in small races.
- Broadcast: Eurosport/TNT (Europe), Peacock/NBC (US), FloBikes (Canada,
  some US), SBS (Australia), GCN+ was shut in 2023.
