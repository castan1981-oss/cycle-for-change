# Glossary — cycling words, fast

**Aero** drag-reducing; **aero bars** clip-on extensions (TT/ultra; banned in
group rides and most gravel races). **Attack** a sudden acceleration to get
away. **Audax / brevet / randonneuring** long self-supported timed rides
(200–1,200 km) with checkpoints; PBP is the big one. **Autobus / grupetto**
the group of non-climbers riding just inside the time cut. **AXS** SRAM's
wireless electronic system.

**Bidon** water bottle. **Bonk** glycogen depletion; the sudden wall.
**Boost** MTB hub spacing (110/148). **Bottom bracket (BB)** the bearing set
the cranks spin in. **Break / breakaway** riders ahead of the peloton.
**Bunny hop** jumping the bike. **Burrito / roll** tube wrapped in a rag.

**Cadence** pedal rpm (80–100 typical). **Cassette** rear cog cluster.
**Cat** USA Cycling category (5 to 1). **Century** 100 miles; metric century
100 km. **Chamois** the pad in bib shorts (and the cream). **Chasse patate**
stuck between the break and the bunch. **Classic** a one-day race; the
Monuments are the five biggest. **Cleat** the shoe plate that clips in.
**Clincher** a tire held by bead hooks; with a tube unless tubeless.
**Clipless** pedals you clip into (no toe clips). **Cobbles / pavé** the
stones of Roubaix and Flanders. **Commissaire** a race official.
**Crit / criterium** short-lap closed-circuit race; the US specialty.
**CX / cyclocross** autumn/winter off-road racing on drop bars with
run-ups and barriers. **CTL/ATL/TSB** fitness / fatigue / form numbers.

**Derailleur** the mech that moves the chain across cogs. **Di2** Shimano's
electronic shifting. **DNF/DNS** did not finish/start. **Domestique** a
helper rider. **Draft** ride in another's slipstream. **Drop** to leave
someone behind; also "drops," the lower part of drop bars. **Dropper** a
height-adjustable seatpost. **DUB** SRAM's crank spindle standard.

**Echelon** diagonal crosswind formation. **Enduro** timed downhill stages,
untimed climbs. **eMTB** electric mountain bike. **Everesting** 8,848 m of
climbing on one hill in one ride.

**Fixie** fixed-gear bike. **Flamme rouge** the red kite at 1 km to go.
**Flat mount** road disc-caliper standard. **Fondo / gran fondo** mass
timed ride. **FTP** functional threshold power. **Full-sus** full
suspension.

**GC** general classification (overall). **Gravel** mixed-surface riding;
also the bike. **Gripper** silicone hem on shorts (the CFC kit's "gripper
patch"). **Groupset / gruppo** the drivetrain + brakes as a set. **GRX**
Shimano gravel groupset.

**Half-wheel** riding slightly ahead of your partner (rude). **Hardtail**
front suspension only. **Head unit** the GPS computer. **Hookless** rims
without bead hooks. **Hour Record** farthest in one hour on a track.
**Hors catégorie (HC)** a climb "beyond category."

**Intervals** structured hard efforts. **ITT** individual time trial.
**IBD** independent bike dealer.

**Keirin** motor-paced track sprint. **Kit** jersey and bibs. **KOM/QOM**
king/queen of the mountains (race jersey or Strava segment best).

**Lanterne rouge** last rider in a Grand Tour. **Lead-out** teammates
delivering a sprinter. **Lugs** cast sleeves joining steel tubes.

**Madison** two-rider relay track race. **Maillot jaune / maglia rosa / la
roja** Tour / Giro / Vuelta leaders' jerseys. **Mechanical** an equipment
failure. **MIPS** rotational-impact helmet liner. **Monument** one of the
five oldest, hardest classics. **Mullet** 29" front / 27.5" rear. **Musette**
feed-zone bag.

**Neutral service** race-provided mechanical support. **No-drop** group ride
that waits. **Normalized power** an intensity-adjusted average.

**Omnium** multi-event track competition. **On your left** the pass call.

**Paceline** single or double line rotating pulls. **Pavé** cobbles.
**Peloton** the main bunch. **Pinch flat / snakebite** rim-strike puncture
of a tube. **Polka dot** Tour mountains jersey. **Presta / Schrader** valve
types (skinny / car). **Prologue** short opening TT. **PSI** tire pressure.
**Pull** a turn at the front.

**Q-factor** stance width between pedals. **Quick-release** lever skewer
(legacy).

**Rainbow jersey** world champion's stripes. **Reach / stack** frame fit
numbers. **Rest day** Grand Tour off day. **Rollers** unfixed indoor
trainer. **Rouleur** strong flat-road rider.

**Sag** suspension settle under rider weight (25–30%). **Sealant** tubeless
latex. **Segment** a Strava timed stretch. **Singletrack** trail one bike
wide. **Sit-in / wheel-suck** drafting without pulling. **Skinsuit** one-piece
kit. **Soigneur** team carer. **SPD / SPD-SL** Shimano 2-bolt / 3-bolt pedals.
**Sprint** the finish; **sprinter** the fast-twitch specialist. **Stage race**
multi-day race. **Stem** bar-to-steerer clamp. **Sticky bottle** an illegal
tow from a team car. **Strava** the social/fitness app; Cycle for Change's
tally reads its API. **Sweeper** the rider at the back of a group ride.

**Tempo** steady hard pace. **Thru-axle** bolt-through wheel axle. **Time
cut** max time behind the winner. **Torque** tightness spec (Nm); also e-bike
motor output. **Track stand** balancing stationary. **Trail (geometry)**
steering feel number. **Trainer** indoor stand. **Transmission** SRAM's
hanger-less derailleur. **TSS** training stress score. **TT** time trial.
**Tubeless** no tube, sealant. **Tubular** sewn-up tire glued to the rim.

**UCI** the world governing body. **UDH** universal derailleur hanger.
**Ultra** very long self-supported events. **Unbound** the biggest gravel
race.

**Velodrome** banked track. **Vuelta** the Spanish Grand Tour.

**W/kg** watts per kilo. **Wheelie / manual** front wheel up with / without
pedaling. **WorldTour** the top pro division. **Worlds** UCI World
Championships.

**XC / XCO** cross-country MTB (Olympic format). **XD / XDR** SRAM freehub
standard.

**Yellow card** UCI conduct sanction (since 2024). **Yellow jersey** Tour
leader.

**Zone 2** easy endurance intensity. **Zwift** the indoor riding game.
