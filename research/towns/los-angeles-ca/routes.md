## Why these

- **The beach path** — Zone 1, the Westside and the coast. The ride for the day you land: flat, car-free, any length. 22 miles one way puts it at "moderate" by the difficulty rule; the editor may want the tile to say flat. The start is a state beach, not a café. Elevation is null because the county page gives none.
- **Latigo – Mulholland – PCH loop** — Zone 2, the Santa Monica Mountains. The canyon climb with a real route page and a Malibu start, so a rider can skip the PCH work zone by driving to the civic center. Hand-off: it shares Mulholland Highway with the **Mulholland ClimbFest** (April 10, 2027, confirmed in `data/calendar-2027.json`, id 44). Hand-off for @shop-scout and @coffee-scout: **Helen's Cycles** keeps its own route library on its site (nine rides, with a 55-mile Latigo version and a 75-mile "PCH to the Rock"); the shop is where those rides leave from.
- **Griffith Park and LA River loop** — Zone 3. The river path and the park in one 26-mile loop, about two-thirds on bike path or car-free road. No fixed start; it's a loop.
- **Griffith Park loop from the Zoo lot** — Zone 3. The 13-mile lap from the free lot by the Zoo. Shortest on the list, but 1,200 feet keeps it at moderate by the rule.
- **Glendora Mountain Road** — Zone 4, the San Gabriels. The climb, with numbers from the club time-trial course. Glendora is outside the city; the brief's Zone 4 is the reason. Hand-off for the calendar: **Lightning Velo's GMR Challenge** ran June 20, 2026 (USAC TT, bikereg) and is not in `data/calendar-2027.json`.
- **GMR to Mt. Baldy loop** — Zone 4. The 6,850-foot day. Hand-off: the calendar's **Mt. Baldy Bicycle Hill Climb** (id 577, `tba`) is on the Baldy side of this loop.
- **The Donut loop** — Zone 5, the South Bay and Palos Verdes. The loop of the Donut Ride. Redondo Beach is outside the city; the brief's Zone 5 is the reason. The group ride itself isn't in `rides.json` for LA and its start wasn't confirmed here — a lead for @community-scout.
- **Nichols Canyon – Mulholland** — the group-ride route, Zone 6 by the brief (Nichols from the Hollywood side), though it starts in Westwood, about 11 miles west of City Hall. It belongs to `los-angeles-ca-la-grange-nichols-canyon-ride` in `rides.json`; the rider can join the ride or do the loop alone. The start is a public square, not a café. Of the other LA rides in `rides.json`, none publishes a fixed route: Different Spokes and the Women's Ride rotate, SFVBC posts a different ride each weekend, and the Venice Electric Light Parade is a social roll.

Hand-offs for @community-scout and @coffee-scout, from lagrange.org/rides (fetched): La Grange runs five weekday rides at 6:30 a.m. from **26th St & San Vicente Blvd, Santa Monica** — Marina Ride (Tue, 26 mi), Mandeville Canyon (Wed, 20 mi), Gravel Wednesday (15 mi, "loose dirt"), Amalfi Loops (Thu, 26 mi), Marina Lite (Fri, 23 mi, recovery). None is in `rides.json`. Whatever is open at 6:30 near that corner is the ride-out coffee. Two day conflicts to settle: the club's Nichols page says "Sunday, 8 AM" while its rides page and our directory say Saturday; the rides page lists the Women's Ride on the 2nd Saturday while our directory says 3rd.

## Couldn't confirm

- **A PCH signature ride north of Santa Monica.** Helen's Cycles lists "PCH to the Rock" (75 mi, 3,150 ft, "PCH all the way to the Rock just past Sycamore Cove", the shop's mainstay endurance ride) with no route link, and "Encinal Canyon–Mulholland" (70 mi, 6,200 ft) the same way. RideWithGPS 33986805 ("Santa Monica to Calabasas and Malibu", 52.6 mi, 4,776 ft) has no roads on the page. Where to look: ask Helen's for the RideWithGPS version of PCH to the Rock; or a Velo Club La Grange or LA Wheelmen collection on RideWithGPS.
- **Start addresses and coordinates** for every start except Raymond Fouquet Square: Will Rogers State Beach (the county page), Civic Center Way in Malibu (the route page's words), the Zoo lot in Griffith Park, GMR at Big Dalton Canyon Rd, "Glendora" and "Redondo Beach" (route page localities only). Where to look: the route pages' first track point (the RideWithGPS `.json` endpoint was blocked here), or the place's own page.
- **The beach path after the fire.** The county page (updated April 25, 2025) still lists Will Rogers State Beach as the north end. No 2026 source was found on the state of the path's north end or of Will Rogers State Beach itself. The PCH work-zone line is from 2026 sources (Caltrans project page, read Sept 30, 2026; Santa Monica Daily Press, May 1, 2026). Where to look: LA County Beaches & Harbors, California State Parks (Will Rogers SB), the Caltrans project page.
- **Crowds and pedestrians on the beach path** — the obvious hazard and no fetched page says it. Where to look: the county's path rules or the Santa Monica city page for its section.
- **Whether GMR's vehicle closures also close it to bikes**, and the current gate status. Where to look: Angeles National Forest alerts, LA County Public Works road closures, the Lightning Velo TT page for 2027.
- **Hazards and water on Latigo, Mulholland Highway, Griffith Park and Palos Verdes** — the route pages give none; water is null everywhere. Where to look: the Mulholland ClimbFest rider guide (Planet Ultra) for water on Mulholland; the Griffith Park page at recreation.parks.lacity.gov for fountains.
- **The Donut Ride group** — day, start, time, and whether it's still running. Where to look: South Bay Wheelmen, Beach Cities Cycling Club, the local shops in Redondo/Hermosa.
- **A gravel or dirt ride.** Helen's Cycles lists four off-road rides (Sullivan Ridge–Topanga Park 34 mi / 4,100 ft; Sullivan Ridge–Westridge 22 mi / 2,500 ft; Paseo Miramar–Topanga Fireroad–Dirt Mulholland 30 mi / 3,900 ft; Sullivan Canyon–Canyonback 22 mi / 2,500 ft) with no links, and La Grange runs Gravel Wednesday (15 mi from 26th & San Vicente). None has a public route page fetched here. Where to look: lagrange.org/gravelwednesday, a RideWithGPS search for "Dirt Mulholland" or "Sullivan Canyon".
- **Nichols Canyon numbers.** The club says 27 miles; the RideWithGPS route says 25.8 miles and 1,942 feet. The club's mileage is used, per the rules; the elevation comes from the RideWithGPS page only. The club's ride day (Saturday vs Sunday) conflicts across its own pages.

- **Phil's Cookie Fondo** (Malibu, Oct 23, 2027, projected) and the **Grand Tour Double Century** (LA Wheelmen, June 27, 2027, projected) — courses not fetched; the ClimbFest is the only 2027 event tied to a route here.
