## Why these

- **LAX** — the airport with every carrier and the closest to the Westside; the LAX-it lot and the off-airport rental shuttle are the two things a rider with a case needs to know before landing.
- **Burbank** — twelve miles from downtown the other way, one building for ten rental counters, and a train station across the street; the better door for Griffith Park, the Valley and Pasadena, and a smaller airport to haul a case through.
- **Long Beach** — Southwest, Delta, Alaska and Hawaiian only; the South Bay and PV door, and a calmer landing for a Phoenix or Boise rider on Southwest or Alaska.
- **Ontario** — listed because a rider coming only for GMR and Baldy lands closer here than anywhere; said plainly that it is the wrong side of the metro for everything else.
- **John Wayne** — listed for completeness with the same warning; every airline a rider would use flies there, but it is 34 miles from downtown and farther from the riding.
- **The four airline notes** — every one read on the airline's own page this month, with the current first- and second-bag fee from the airline's fee page so the rent-versus-bring math has real numbers. Alaska and Southwest both moved to $45/$55 in April 2026; American to $50/$60 in May 2026. That is the hand-off to the editor for every other town.
- **Bike Shop LA, Helen's Santa Monica, Bike Attack** — lifted whole from `shops.md`; the only shop in the city that says it receives a shipped bike, and the only two that rent a road bike a visitor would ride.
- **Metro's bike page** — the one agency page that answers rail, bus rack, folding and e-bike rules in one place, dated July 2026.
- **Metro Bike Share** — priced from its own FAQ; listed as the errand bike it is.
- **CVC 21760 via public.law** — the statute site blocks fetches; public.law carries the current text with the January 1, 2023 amendment. CalBike's page (modified August 2026) is the plain-language layer and is where the e-bike classes and sidewalk rule are quoted from; the DMV page carries the helmet and lane-position lines.
- **LAMC 56.15** — the city code itself, from the city's code library, for "LA allows sidewalk riding."
- **LAist, BikinginLA, Crosstown** — the traffic read comes from LAPD figures as reported by three local outlets, with the controller's audit for the Vision Zero line. Not vibes.
