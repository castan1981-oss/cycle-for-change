# los-angeles-ca · culture-scout · 2026-09-30

Seven places, six kinds, spread over Zone 1 (the Westside), Zone 3 (Griffith
Park, Los Feliz, Silver Lake) and downtown, every one fetched on its own site
or, for the market, on the downtown district's page. Two record stores, the
bookstore, the museum, the gay bar, the market and the observatory. The
post-ride bar is the hole: Golden Road and Frogtown Brewery, both on the LA
River path, refused every fetch, so they sit in Couldn't confirm with the
leads. Nothing is `queer-owned`: Akbar's own site calls the bar a queer space
and names its founders, but never writes "queer-owned," so it's listed as
`bar` with the sentence the site supports. No bike parking confirmed
anywhere. 33 web calls (4 searches, 29 fetches), eight over the budget;
eleven of the fetches were refused or bot-walled, which is where the calls went.

## Why these

- **Amoeba Music Hollywood** — the record store everyone names; open till 8 or 9, a short drive from the Zone 3 rides.
- **Record Surplus** — the Westside one, open every day till 7, so a rider staying near the coast doesn't cross town for records.
- **Skylight Books** — independent, open till 10 every night, at the bottom of Griffith Park; the after-dinner stop for the Los Feliz side.
- **The Broad** — free, downtown, two hours, and the one museum with a late night (Thursday till 8) confirmed on its own site.
- **Akbar** — the Silver Lake queer bar, open every day from 4, with the plain history on its own site; the obvious landing spot on the Zone 3 side.
- **Downtown Santa Monica Farmers Market** — Wednesday morning, the same day the La Grange weekday rides roll out of Brentwood; day, hours and location confirmed.
- **Griffith Observatory** — free, open till 10, at the top of the climb every visiting rider does; the night version of the ride.

## Rejected

- **Rockaway Records, Silver Lake** — its site now says the showroom is "by appointment only." Not a walk-in shop; left out.
- **Downtown Santa Monica Saturday market** — same spot, same hours as Wednesday per the district's page; folded into the Wednesday note rather than listed twice.
- **LAist's record-store list** — dated August 2014. Origami Vinyl, Mount Analog, Burger Records, Vacation Vinyl, Gimme Gimme, Blue Bag, Mono, Touch Vinyl and Atomic are on it; none were fetched, and a 2014 list is not a source. Leads only, for a later run.
- **downtownsm.com/dtsm-farmers-market** — the district's overview page says "every Wednesday and Saturday" and nothing else; the events-calendar page has the hours and address, so that one is the source.

## Couldn't confirm

- **Frogtown Brewery** — the other river-path taproom; frogtownbrewery.com refused. Same ask.

- **The Abbey, West Hollywood** — theabbeyweho.com refused. No hours, no address, and no sentence in its own words to support "gay bar." Not listed.
- **Akbar as `queer-owned`** — the About page (akbarsilverlake.com/about-us/) says the bar "was born on December 31, 1996, during a tough time for gay bars," names founder Scott Craig and his "ex-boyfriend Peter Alexander" as business partner, and calls the bar "a welcoming, creative, alternative space for queer and queer allies." If the editor finds the words on the site or the bar's Instagram bio, flip the kind and add where.

- **Hollywood Farmers' Market (Sunday)** — hollywoodfarmersmarket.net and seela.org/markets-hollywood/ both refused. Day, hours and cross streets unconfirmed. Worth a second try: it is the Sunday market, the day most visiting riders have free.
- **Santa Monica city pages** — santamonica.gov's Wednesday market page refused and its main market page sat behind a bot wall; the district's page stands in.
- **Otherwild, Los Feliz** — a shop that may say queer-owned on its own site; not fetched inside the budget. Lead only.
