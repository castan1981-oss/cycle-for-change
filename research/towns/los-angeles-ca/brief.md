## The town

- id `los-angeles-ca` · Los Angeles, California · Los Angeles County · America/Los_Angeles
- Centre for distances: **downtown, 34.0522, -118.2437** (City Hall). The
  riding centre is west of there; say which zone every pick sits in.
- `kind: destination`. No event in `data/events/` yet; the calendar has three.

## Who it's for

A rider from somewhere smaller landing at LAX or Burbank with a bike case,
or deciding whether to. They have three to seven days, a rental car or not,
and they want the coast, one big climb, and to find a Saturday ride. They
are not here for the sights.

## Zones (spread the picks across them)

1. **The Westside and the coast** — Santa Monica / Venice / Marina del Rey /
   Pacific Palisades; the Marvin Braude bike path; the PCH north to Malibu.
2. **The Santa Monica Mountains** — Topanga, Latigo, Mulholland, the Rock
   Store, Piuma, Stunt; the canyon climbs off PCH and from the Valley side.
3. **Griffith Park and the LA River** — the observatory loop, the river
   path (Frogtown, Elysian Valley), Silver Lake / Los Feliz / Echo Park.
4. **The San Gabriels via Pasadena** — Angeles Crest (Hwy 2), GMR (Glendora
   Mountain Road), Mt. Wilson; the big-climb zone.
5. **The South Bay and Palos Verdes** — the beach path south, PV loop, the
   Donut ride; Long Beach has its own hub and 10 rides (`ca-long-beach`).
6. **The Valley** — Northridge / Burbank; Nichols Canyon from the Hollywood
   side; SFVBC.

## Watch for

- The Palisades and Eaton fires (January 2025) changed the Westside coast
  and Altadena. Route pages and shop pages from before that may be stale;
  a route through the Palisades or up to Altadena needs a 2026 source.
- PCH north of Santa Monica has had closures and rebuilds; the hazards
  line on any PCH route must say what's true now, with a 2026 source.
- LA is big. "Los Angeles" listings should sit inside the city or the
  Westside cities (Santa Monica, Culver City, West Hollywood, Beverly
  Hills). Long Beach and Pasadena have their own future guides; a pick
  there needs a reason.
