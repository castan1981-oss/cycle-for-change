## Who it's for

A rider flying into SFO or Oakland with a bike case, or renting one here,
with three to six days. They want the bridge and the Marin Headlands, one
proper climb (Tam, or Skyline on the Peninsula), a Saturday group ride, and
a place to sleep that takes the bike inside. Some are here for Cycle to Zero
or the Recovery Ride and need the days around it sorted.

## Zones (spread the picks across them)

1. **The Presidio, the bridge and the Marin Headlands** — Golden Gate Bridge
   bike side rules, Hawk Hill / Conzelman, Sausalito, Paradise Loop, Tiburon.
2. **Marin** — Mill Valley, Mt. Tamalpais (Panoramic / Ridgecrest / Seven
   Sisters), Fairfax, Alpine Dam, Point Reyes Station (the classic long day).
3. **Golden Gate Park, the ocean side and Lake Merced** — the car-free
   Great Highway park (check its current name and status: a 2026 source),
   JFK Promenade, Lake Merced loop.
4. **The Peninsula** — Skyline Blvd, Kings Mountain, Old La Honda, Woodside
   / Portola Valley loop. The Palo Alto rides hub (`/rides/ca/palo-alto/`,
   21 rides) is there; link it, don't re-list it.
5. **The East Bay by BART** — Berkeley hills, Grizzly Peak, the Three Bears,
   Mt. Diablo. Bike-on-BART rules matter here (logistics).
6. **Where a visitor sleeps and eats** — the Castro, the Mission, Hayes
   Valley, North Beach / the Marina (close to the bridge).

## Watch for

- Golden Gate Bridge: which sidewalk bikes use on which days and hours, and
  the bridge's own published rules — a 2026 source only.
- The Great Highway: closed to cars and made a park in 2025 (Prop K). Name
  and hours from the city's or the park's own 2026 page.
- Muni and BART bike rules (folding bikes, rush-hour limits, bike cars), the
  ferries (Larkspur, Sausalito, Tiburon) as the ride-home option — current
  pages only.
- Car break-ins: a bike left on a car rack is a common loss. The logistics
  section can say "don't leave a bike on a car" if a source (SFPD, the city,
  a shop) says it; no scare copy without one.
- Hills: Twin Peaks and the city streets are short and steep; say so in route
  notes where a source does.
- Fog and wind: summer is cold on the coast. `best_months` from a source.
