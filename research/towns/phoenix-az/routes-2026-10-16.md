# phoenix-az · route-scout · 2026-10-16

Four new routes for the Phoenix guide, plus one reserve. With the eight
already in `data/towns/phoenix-az.json` that makes twelve. They fill the
gaps the Oct 3 run left open: a road climb north of Cave Creek, the first
gravel route in the guide, a path ride in Tempe, and a first dirt ride. The
reserve is a second Tempe path ride.

How this run went: 37 searches and fetches, over the budget of 30. With the
five entries added, the Phoenix file (13 routes) passes
`node tools/verify-town.js --no-fetch` with 0 fails. The only new warnings
are the expected "no lat/lon" ones. One page was blocked (the McDowell Sonoran Conservancy's Brown's Ranch
page; the permission request timed out). Brown's Ranch has the same facts
from the City of Scottsdale's preserve page instead. Two GPX links turned up
and neither holds up: MTB Project's GPX URL returns its HTML trail page, not
a file, and Komoot's needs a login. So `links.gpx` is null on all five.

Read this before you use Brown's Ranch: **the City of Scottsdale says the
Brown's Ranch Trailhead is closed for improvements, with a grand reopening
expected in late October** (preserve page, read Oct 16, 2026). It is in the
hazards line. Re-check before the guide goes live.

Heat is in every hazard line, in the guide's existing wording: summer highs
average 104 to 106 F (PJAMM's South Mountain page, the source the other
routes use). Brown's Ranch also carries the city's own heat guidance.

`miles` and `elevation_gain_ft` follow the route-scout rounding (nearest
50 ft). The exact figures are in each description. The two Tempe loops keep
their half miles (7.5 and 23.5), because on a short loop half a mile
matters. Round them if the editor prefers whole miles.

## Why these

- **Tempe Town Lake loop (pick 1).** This is the easy ride in Tempe, the
  zone the guide most needs. It's flat and paved, with water and restrooms
  at the start, and you can ride it the evening you land. It also links the
  guide's two existing path routes: the north bank meets the Indian Bend Wash
  path. Easy by the rule: 7.5 miles of path, no climbing. The figure is Tempe
  Tourism's. The City of Tempe says only "more than seven miles of paths."
  The route link is TrailLink's Rio Salado map, the same kind of source as the
  Indian Bend Wash entry. Hand-offs: the token names the Tuesday-night social
  ride from R.T. O'Sullivan's
  (`scottsdale-az-phoenix-hiking-biking-group-tuesday-night-ride`), which
  loops the lake. It's not set as `ride_slug` because the ride starts in
  Scottsdale, not here. Tempe Tourism says Valley Metro Rail stops are near
  the lake, and it names hotel loaner bikes at Canopy, Moxy and Graduate
  Tempe and rentals at The Bicycle Cellar. Those are leads for
  @logistics-scout and @stay-scout, not checked here.
- **Brown's Ranch, The Long Way Around (pick 2).** The first-time dirt ride.
  MTB Project rates it easy to intermediate and says nothing on it is
  technical. Signposts at every junction, a 1 percent average grade, water
  and restrooms at the trailhead. By the fixed rule it's moderate, not easy:
  the Oct 3 run graded Trail 100 and the McDowell track moderate because
  singletrack isn't "path or quiet road," and this keeps to that. Start
  lat/lon is the City of Scottsdale's (the land manager): 33° 45' 41.70" N,
  111° 50' 32.06" W, converted to 33.7616, -111.8422. MTB Project gives
  33.761141, -111.843325, about 0.1 mile away. Hand-offs: the Desert
  Foothills Mountain Bike Association is the local club on MTB Project's page,
  and its Saturday rides (`cave-creek-az-dfmba-saturday-group-rides`) rotate
  trailheads. Nothing confirmed that they use this loop, so no `ride_slug`.
  For Robert: the city says **group hikes or rides in the preserve need a
  special use permit** (7 to 10 business days). That matters if he ever leads
  a ride here.
- **Bartlett Dam Road (pick 3).** The road classic north of the city: a
  9-mile climb into the Tonto from the lake. Graded moderate as a climb
  alone (2,004 ft at 3.9 percent, no sustained 6 percent), the same way
  South Mountain is graded. A day from Cave Creek adds the 13 miles each
  way between the FR 24 junction and the lake; no fetched page gives
  numbers for that whole ride, so none are given. The start
  is a store and restaurant at the lake; a lead for @eat-scout, not
  confirmed open. Picked over the Bush Highway to Saguaro Lake because the
  Usery Loop route already rides the Bush Highway along the Salt River.
- **Seven Springs Road to Bloody Basin Road (pick 4).** The guide's first
  gravel route; the Oct 3 run couldn't find one. It shares its start with
  Bartlett, at the Bartlett Lake turn-off. Graded hard on the page's one-way
  numbers (30 mi, 3,330 ft). Most riders ride it out and back, and the
  author's own figure for that is over 6,000 feet, which is epic by the rule.
  The editor may want the tile to say "one way." The source is the weakest
  of the five: a 2016 Gravelmap route, with rider comments from 2020 and
  2022. The Forest Service pages back the parts that matter most (gravel
  road, narrow, blind curves, no water). Swap it for the reserve if a 2016
  page is too old for the guide.
- **Tempe Beach Park to West Ray Road loop (reserve).** A second Tempe ride
  for riders who find the lake loop too short: 23.5 miles, mostly cycleway,
  same start and water. It's the reserve, not a pick, because it's a
  machine-made Komoot "smarttour" (like the guide's Indian Bend Wash
  Greenbelt loop). Komoot doesn't name the paths it uses, and about 3 miles
  are on streets it doesn't name. Use it instead of Seven Springs if the
  editor wants two Tempe routes and no 2016 source. Hand-off: PMBC's Saturday
  ride (`tempe-az-pmbc-saturday-cycling`) starts at Kiwanis Park, on this
  loop. It's named by token, not `ride_slug`, because PMBC rotates more than
  a dozen routes.
- **Calendar hand-off.** Belgian Waffle Ride Arizona (calendar id 24,
  March 7, 2027) is listed as "Cave Creek / Fountain Hills." No course was
  fetched, so it isn't linked to Bartlett or Seven Springs.

## Rejected

  2023). It's one person's ride file, not a route a visitor would follow. Its
  public GPX exists (`/trips/123442724.gpx?sub_format=track`) but belongs to
  that ride, so it isn't used as Bartlett's GPX.

  (5.6 km) with about half a mile of gravel. It's not a bike route, and its GPX
  needs a login.
- **Reach 11 Recreation Area (City of Phoenix).** The city page says 18
  miles of multi-use trails in a 7-mile-long strip along the CAP canal. It
  gives no trail names, no bike rules, no trailhead, no water, and "call for
  hours." No route page was found, so no route.
- **MTB Project "Brown's Ranch Perimeter Loop"** (15.5 mi, 607 ft). Rated
  Intermediate, a step harder than The Long Way Around, so it doesn't fill the
  first-dirt-ride slot. **West Side Loop** (19.5 mi, 706 ft) and
  **Gooseneck-Pemberton Lollipop** (15.2 mi, 599 ft, from Rio Verde) are both
  Easy/Intermediate. They'd be the next dirt routes for this preserve.
- **Tempe Tourism's Crosscut Canal, Kyrene Canal and Western Canal via
  Kiwanis Park rides.** No distances published and no route pages. Used only
  as background.
- **Tempe Bikeway Map legend (tempe.gov PDF).** Only the key loaded (symbols,
  path widths), not the map. Used for the 10-foot path width.
- **Bush Highway to Saguaro Lake.** Not pursued. The existing Usery Loop
  route already rides the Bush Highway along the Salt River.
- **regroupwithus.com.** Not used as a source or a link, per the brief.

## Couldn't confirm

- **Brown's Ranch Trailhead reopening.** The city expects late October. Its
  page doesn't give the year, but it was read on Oct 16, 2026. The
  Conservancy's own Brown's Ranch page (mcdowellsonoran.org/browns/) was
  blocked. While the trailhead is closed, the Conservancy lists **Granite
  Mountain Trailhead, 31402 N. 136th St.** and **Pima Dynamite Trailhead,
  28777 N Pima Rd.** in the same northern region. No page said how to join
  this loop from either one.
- **GPX files.** MTB Project lists `https://www.mtbproject.com/trail/gpx/7035760`,
  but loading it returns the HTML trail page, not a file (probably needs a
  login). Komoot's GPX needs a login. Gravelmap lists GPX and TCX downloads
  with no URL in the page. PJAMM's "Download Route" button had no URL.
- **Bartlett: where the top is.** PJAMM labels the finish "Horseshoe Dam,"
  3,682 ft, and says the road turns to dirt half a mile on. Whether the top
  is on Bartlett Dam Rd itself or up the Horseshoe Dam Rd branch isn't clear
  from the text. Check PJAMM's map before the guide names the finish.
  Also unconfirmed: whether The Cove and Oar House is open and when, and
  whether it sits at the marina address the Forest Service gives (20808 E
  Bartlett Dam Rd). No page described the shoulder.
- **Seven Springs: the start and the road today.** The ranger station at the
  Bartlett turn-off comes only from the 2016 Gravelmap page. The Forest
  Service's Cave Creek Ranger District page has no office address. No
  current FR 24 road report was found. The page's own text says "95%
  gravel," but its stats split it 54 percent gravel, 32 percent dirt and
  13 percent pavement; the stats are used.
- **Start lat/lon** for the Tempe loops, Bartlett and Seven Springs. No
  fetched page gave coordinates.
- **Tempe Beach Park's address.** The City of Tempe lists "Mill Ave. and Rio
  Salado Pkwy." TrailLink says 54 W. Rio Salado Parkway. The city's is used.
- **Tempe: the bridges.** TrailLink names a bike and pedestrian bridge with
  the Tempe Center for the Arts at its south end. Komoot names the Elmore
  Pedestrian Bridge at the west end, beside the dam. They are probably the
  same bridge; not confirmed.
- **Tempe: the McClintock link.** The City of Tempe's "Rio Salado Multi-use
  Path – Indian Bend Wash to McClintock" project page gives no status or
  finish date. If it's still being built, part of the 7.5-mile loop near
  McClintock may be detoured. Ask the city's transportation department.
- **Elevation for the Tempe Town Lake loop.** No page publishes it.
- **Kiwanis Park amenities** (on the reserve loop). Not on the city page
  that loaded.
