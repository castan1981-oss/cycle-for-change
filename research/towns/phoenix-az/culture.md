## 2. Why these

- **Stinkweeds**: the Phoenix record store, open since 1987, on the light rail. It opens at 11, so it's a stop after the ride.
- **Changing Hands**: the Valley's independent bookstore. Tempe is the home store. The Phoenix store has a bar, so a rider can sit with a beer and a book.
- **Boycott Bar**: says in its own words that it's lesbian-owned and LGBTQ+ owned, on the Melrose strip. Open late, and from 10 a.m. Sunday.
- **Heard Museum**: the museum worth two hours in Phoenix. It's on the light rail, has free parking, and opens every day in riding season.
- **Pedal Haus**: a brewery its own page says was started for cyclists. A patio on Roosevelt Row and one on Mill Ave in Tempe, near ASU.
- **Old Town Scottsdale Farmers Market**: runs Saturday mornings October to April, which is the winter-visitor season.
- **Scottsdale Stadium**: spring training in late February and March is the thing to do when a training week lands then.

- **Old Town Scottsdale Farmers Market address.** The operator's page (arizonacommunityfarmersmarkets.com/old-town-scottsdale) wouldn't load. Two City of Scottsdale listings dated Oct 3, 2026 give 7495 East Bennie Gonzales Way and say "NEW LOCATION starting October 3rd!" A third city listing (community.scottsdaleaz.gov/scottsdaleaz_main/260214108) gives 3939 N Drinkwater Blvd. Older listings name 3806 N Brown Ave, the lot it left. Confirm on the operator's page. - **Scottsdale Stadium address.** Tempe Tourism (Jan 6, 2026) gives 7408 E. Osborn Road, Scottsdale, AZ 85251. Neither the city's page (scottsdaleaz.gov/scottsdale-stadium) nor the Giants' ticket page (mlb.com/giants/tickets/spring-training) would load, so the field is null. Game-by-game dates at the park are also unconfirmed: cactusleague.com/schedule.php gave two different lists on two reads. The note uses only the league's first game (Feb 19, 2027) and last game (Mar 20, 2027). Ticket prices unknown.
- **First Friday art walk, Roosevelt Row (for the editor to weigh, not for the guide as written).** Axios (May 1, 2026): the vendor market and street closures were dropped after "a string of ugly and violent incidents," including shootings. The art walk is still monthly, run by the Roosevelt Row CDC, and draws 20,000+. Phoenix New Times reports two people shot at a March First Friday (the fetch didn't give the year). It's the obvious Roosevelt Row evening, but it has a stated safety history, so it stays out until the editor decides.
- **Phoenix General** (Roosevelt Row shop). Phoenix Magazine (June 2024) listed it as LGBTQ-owned at 214 E. Roosevelt St with no owner quote. That's now Pedal Haus's Phoenix address, so it has probably moved or closed. phxgeneral.com wouldn't load.

- **Grassrootz Bookstore** (Phoenix). Turned up in search (Wikipedia). Not fetched.
