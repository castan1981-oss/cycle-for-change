## Who it's for

A rider coming for the winter — November to April — from somewhere cold:
a training week, a team camp, a month away. Some fly into PHX with a bike
case, some rent. They want South Mountain at dawn, the Scottsdale / Fountain
Hills / Rio Verde loops, the Bush Highway to Saguaro Lake, a Saturday group
ride to join, and somewhere to stay with the bike in the room. They also need
the summer truth if they come in July.

## Watch for

- **Heat** is the hazard line on every route from May to October: start at
  dawn, water, the parks' own heat closures (Phoenix closes some trails on
  extreme-heat days — City of Phoenix 2026 pages). No scare copy; facts with
  sources.
- South Mountain Summit Road: car-free hours / Silent Sundays — the city's
  current page.
- Valley Metro light rail and bus bike rules — current pages.
- The 101/202 and the canals: canal paths have gaps and street crossings; say
  so only from a source.
- Flats: goatheads and glass. Only with a local shop source.
