# phoenix-az · coffee-scout · 2026-10-04 · the Gainey Village coffee stop

One question: where do the Scottsdale Cycling rides (Gainey Tuesday, Gainey
Thursday, the Saturday "Fuss Buss") get their coffee, and is anything at Gainey
Village open at 5–6 am? Budget: 10 searches, all used. Fetches that failed are
listed at the foot.

## The short answer

- **There is no café at Gainey Village any more.** The Coffee Bean & Tea Leaf at
  8877 N Scottsdale Rd Ste 408 — the one riders used — is gone. Three signals
  agree (the center's own directory, the chain's own store list, a Yellow Pages
  listing marked CLOSED). Nothing at the corner opens before 7. **Confidence:
  high.**
- **The club does not name its coffee stop anywhere public.** Only the Saturday
  ride says there is one: "Coffee stop at the end, always mandatory." Its routes
  change weekly, so the stop probably does too. The Tuesday and Thursday event
  pages say nothing about coffee. **Where the stop is: unconfirmed.** No rider
  chatter about it turned up (Bike Forums 403; nothing on Reddit or TrainerRoad
  came back in search).
- **The coffee is after, not before.** The rides roll at 5:30 (Saturday now 6:00)
  and nothing nearby is open. The nearest café that is open when the Thursday
  ride gets back (about 7:15 after ~29 miles) is Village Coffee Roastery, 1.6
  miles east, open 6 am to 2 pm daily per its own site (fetched this run — the
  last scout couldn't open it). Hash Kitchen, in the Shops Gainey Village at the
  start corner, opens at 7 every day; it's a breakfast restaurant, not a café.

Two things for other scouts, found on the way:
- **@ride-verifier:** the Fuss Buss Strava event now reads **6:00 AM** Saturday,
  next date Oct 10, from "8977 N Scottsdale Rd Suite 506". `rides.json` has it
  at 5:30. A `start_times` row or a re-check is due.
- **@eat-scout:** Hash Kitchen, Unit 308, 8777 N Scottsdale Rd, 7 am daily — the
  only thing at the start corner open when the Thursday ride ends.

## Conclusion

1. Nothing at Gainey Village is open at 5–6 am, and there is no café there at
   all now that the Coffee Bean is closed. **High confidence.**
2. The club's "mandatory" coffee stop is on the Saturday ride, at the end, and
   unnamed; with routes changing weekly it is likely not one fixed café. The
   Tuesday and Thursday rides say nothing about coffee in public. **Where any of
   them actually stop: unconfirmed.** Robert rides Gainey Thursday and can
   settle this in one sentence.
3. For the guide, the honest line is: the Gainey rides leave in the dark with no
   coffee at the corner; the nearest café open when you get back is Village
   Coffee Roastery (6 am); Hash Kitchen at the corner opens at 7. **Medium-high
   confidence** on the hours (own pages, fetched), **no** confirmation that the
   club goes to either.
