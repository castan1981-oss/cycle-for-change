## Who it's for

A rider coming to Scottsdale for the Tour de Scottsdale (April 8–10, 2027),
for a winter training week, or for a weekend of the Valley's best road loops.
Some fly into PHX with a bike case, some rent, some drive in from Tucson or
California. They want the loop around the McDowells through Rio Verde and
Fountain Hills (the Tour's 54-mile course circles the same mountains), a
Saturday group ride to join, the Greenbelt for an easy day, dirt at Brown's
Ranch and McDowell Mountain Park, a shop that can fix or rent, and a bed near
the start with the bike in the room.

## Watch for

- **Heat** is the hazard line on every route from May to October: start at
  dawn, water, the preserve's own heat closures (City of Scottsdale pages).
  No scare copy; facts with sources.
- Pima Rd and Scottsdale Rd north: bike lanes but fast traffic; say so only
  from a source (the city's bike map, a club's route page).
- Rio Verde Dr: the long way round has no services between Fountain Hills
  and Dynamite — water from a source (a club page, the Tour's own notes).
- Flats: goatheads and glass, with a local shop source only.
- The McDowell Sonoran Preserve: trailhead hours, which trailheads have
  water, the heat-closure rule — the city's own pages.
- Valley Metro bus bike rules (no light rail in Scottsdale — say so).
- Nothing on the page says "bike-friendly" without a written policy.
