@culture-scout · 2026-10-20

Seven picks: five in Old Town, one in North Scottsdale (the preserve), one south
of Old Town (the pool). Each note names its area. No distances in the notes: I
had no fetched coordinates for any pick, so the editor can add "about N miles
from Old Town" after geocoding. The two Scottsdale entries already on the
Phoenix guide (the farmers market, Scottsdale Stadium) were re-verified; the
market has a new address and the stadium now has one. No Scottsdale business
that says it is queer-owned turned up, and the one bar the brief asked about,
BS West, closed in January 2024, so there is no `queer-owned` entry and no
queer bar on this guide. WebSearch: 12 used. Fourteen direct fetches were
blocked on permission (listed under "Couldn't confirm" with what each would
settle).

## 2. Why these

- **Gateway Trailhead**: the preserve's front door with water, restrooms and shade, free, open sunrise to sunset. A 4.5-mile loop is the right length for legs that have already ridden. The city's page (Sep 30, 2026) is the source for hours, amenities and the no-e-bikes rule.
- **Eldorado pool**: the one city pool the city itself names as year-round (its Chaparral page, May 2026, sends winter lap swimmers to Cactus, Eldorado or McDowell Mountain Ranch). $6 a day, 13 lanes, heated in winter. The swim after a hot ride.
- **Old Town Scottsdale Farmers Market**: the Saturday morning stop after a dawn ride, in season exactly when riders are in town (October through June). Re-verified: the city's own news (Nov 19, 2025) confirms the move to the City Hall lot for 2026–27, which is what the Phoenix guide's note already says.
- **Scottsdale Stadium**: spring training is what a March training week is really about. Re-verified: the Giants' own ballpark page gives the address the Phoenix guide left null.
- **The Poisoned Pen**: Old Town's independent bookstore, open until 7 on weeknights, with a Southwest section. The closest thing to a cycling shelf is the Southwest literature; nothing cycling-specific is on its About page.
- **Western Spirit**: the museum worth two hours in Scottsdale, open seven days in riding season, Thursday evenings November through April, with free parking under it. Price and hours from its own page, modified July 8, 2026.
- **Goldwater Brewing**: the Old Town taproom, a family brewery, open from 1 p.m. Hours and address are from two tourism-board listings (Experience Scottsdale, Visit Arizona, both © 2026) because the brewery's own site was blocked; see "Couldn't confirm."

## 3. Rejected

- **BS West** (7125 E. Fifth Ave., Old Town). Closed. azfamily (Jan 2, 2024): the owners announced the closure that day and the "final goodbye" was Sunday, January 7, 2024; it was "Scottsdale's only nightclub catering to the LGBTQ community" for 35 years. Phoenix New Times' location page flags it "Closed." Its own site (bswest.com) was blocked, so I never read what it said; it doesn't matter now. Nothing to list.
- **A queer-owned Scottsdale business.** None found that says so in its own words. New Times' "8 best LGBTQ bars in metro Phoenix" lists one Scottsdale bar (BS West, now closed) and seven in Phoenix. The nearest bar that says it is LGBTQ-owned is Boycott Bar on the Phoenix guide (Melrose District).
- **A Scottsdale record store.** New Times' metro list (2019, updated since) has no store in Scottsdale: "The Record Room was originally in Old Town Scottsdale but moved to north Phoenix in 2016." Stinkweeds (12 W Camelback Rd, Phoenix) on the Phoenix guide is the Valley's record store; link there.
- **SMoCA, Scottsdale Museum of Contemporary Art** (7374 East Second Street, Scottsdale, AZ 85251). One museum is the ask and Western Spirit opens every day; SMoCA is the swap if the editor wants contemporary art instead. Verified from its own Plan Your Visit page (© 2026): Wednesday 11 to 5, Thursday 11 to 7, Friday to Sunday 11 to 5, closed Monday and Tuesday; $16, $13 for students, seniors, veterans, military and educators, free 18 and under; "pay what you wish on Thursdays and every second Saturday." Parking answer wasn't in the page text.
- **Chaparral Aquatic Center** (5445 N. Hayden Road, Scottsdale, AZ 85250, 480-312-2361). The city page (May 21, 2026) says "Chaparral Pool is closed for the season": a summer pool, 16 lanes, 25 yards, open when riders aren't here. Same fees as Eldorado.
- **R.T. O'Sullivan's Sports Grill** (7919 E Thomas Rd #101). The start of `scottsdale-az-phoenix-hiking-biking-group-tuesday-night-ride`, already in rides.json. A sports grill, not a bar pick; @eat-scout's call.
- **Village Tavern** (8787 N Scottsdale Rd). Already under Restaurants on the Phoenix guide.
- **Experience Scottsdale's brewery story page** and the other Old Town bars it names: not fetched. One bar is enough.
- **Goldwater's Tempe, Mesa and planned Gilbert taprooms** (New Times): outside Scottsdale.
