## Who it's for

A rider who comes for the winter — a week or a month of dry roads, the Loop
and Mount Lemmon — or for El Tour de Tucson in November. Many fly into TUS or
drive down from Phoenix (PHX is ~112 miles). They want the long climb, the
car-free path for an easy day, a fast group ride to test their legs, and a
place that takes the bike inside.

## Zones (spread the picks across them)

1. **Mount Lemmon / Catalina Highway** — the climb from the desert floor to
   Summerhaven; the cookie stop at the top; Windy Point.
2. **The Loop** — the Chuck Huckelberry Loop, Rillito and Santa Cruz River
   paths, the Mercado / west side, the Rillito near the university.
3. **Saguaro National Park East and Old Spanish Trail** — Cactus Forest Loop
   Drive, Colossal Cave, Vail.
4. **The Tucson Mountains and the west** — Gates Pass, Kinney Road, Saguaro
   West, McCain Loop.
5. **North: Oro Valley, Catalina State Park, Tortolita** — Oracle Rd, the
   Shootout's terrain.
6. **South: Madera Canyon and Green Valley**; mountain bikers: Sweetwater,
   Fantasy Island, the 50-Year Trail, Mount Lemmon trails.

  a shop says so.
