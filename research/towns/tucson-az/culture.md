# tucson-az · culture-scout · 2026-10-03

Six places, five kinds: the record store, the bookstore, the museum, two bars
and the market. Downtown and Fourth Avenue carry four of them, the market sits
on the Loop at Rillito Park (Zone 2) and the museum is out on Kinney Road
(Zone 4). Four are confirmed on the business's own pages (Wooden Tooth, IBT's,
Pueblo Vida, the Desert Museum). Two are confirmed only on listings because
their own sites would not load in this session: Antigone Books (GayTucson,
June 2026, plus NewPages and Local First Arizona) and the Rillito market
(Visit Tucson, a 2026 vendor calendar, Tucson Foodie). Both are flagged in
Couldn't confirm. Nothing is `queer-owned`: no business I could fetch says so
in its own words. IBT's says it is Tucson's gay bar and is listed as `bar`.
One bike-parking line confirmed: Pueblo Vida has racks and will take the bike
inside. Search ran out (8 of 8 used); the rest was done by fetch, and about a
dozen fetches were refused (permission withdrawn, robots.txt, 403, 404).

## Why these

- **Wooden Tooth Records (Congress Street)** — the record store, downtown where most visiting riders sleep, with a bar inside and open till 10 on Friday and Saturday nights.
- **Antigone Books** — the Fourth Avenue bookstore, independent since 1973, a short walk from downtown; a Sunday afternoon after the long ride.
- **Arizona-Sonora Desert Museum** — the one museum worth two hours, and it is out on the Zone 4 roads, so it fits a west-side day.
- **Pueblo Vida Brewing Company** — the post-ride bar that says in writing it will take the bike inside. Downtown, open from noon on weekends.
- **IBT's Bar + Food** — Tucson's gay bar since 1985, open every day from noon, with a patio; the obvious landing spot on Fourth Avenue.
- **Heirloom Farmers Market at Rillito Park** — on the Loop itself, Sunday morning, with shade and restrooms; ride to it, eat, ride on.

## Rejected

- **Wooden Tooth Records, 7th Street** (426 E. 7th St.) — same shop, earlier hours (noon to 6 weekdays), no bar. Folded into the Congress Street note rather than listed twice.
- **Zia Records, Speedway** (3370 E Speedway Blvd, per Keep Them Spinning, July 2026) — a regional chain store in midtown; Wooden Tooth is the independent one downtown. Not fetched on its own site.
- **Desert Island Records** (2930 E Broadway), **Old Paint Records** (76 W Washington St, downtown), **Hurricane Records** (636 N 4th Ave) — named in Keep Them Spinning's July 2026 list. Leads only; none fetched on its own page. Old Paint and Hurricane are both close to the downtown hotels if the editor wants a second record store.
- **Tucson Foodie's "Beer on the Loop" guide** (Nov 2023, updated March 2024) — 13 bars and breweries by Loop segment, but no hours, distances or bike-parking lines, and none fetched on their own pages. Leads: Westbound (Mercado, beside Transit Cycles), Dragoon Brewing (W Grant Rd), Tucson Hop Shop (N Dodge Blvd, Rillito), Catalina Brewing (Marana).
- **Borderlands Brewing, Moto Sonora, 1912 Brewing** — named in a June 2024 mountain-bike Substack with nothing about bikes; Pueblo Vida was the one with a bike line, and its own page confirmed it.
- **La Suprema Works** — Tucson Love Letter (Feb 2026) calls it a queer-owned co-working and events space. A co-working space is not a rider's afternoon, and the only link is an Instagram account I couldn't fetch.
- **Celestia Collective, Monsoon Mystics** — tattoo, piercing and witch shops that host queer events, per Tucson Love Letter. Not a rider's afternoon.
- **Feast** — a restaurant (Local First Arizona calls it LGBTQ+-friendly). Restaurant, so @eat-scout's, and "friendly" is not a field.
- **Everywhere Is Queer, "7 Queer-Owned Businesses To Visit In Tucson"** — the page returned 404.

## Couldn't confirm

- **Antigone Books — own site.** antigonebooks.com didn't load in this session (permission withdrawn, not a dead site). The address and hours in Findings come from GayTucson (modified June 2, 2026); NewPages (Aug 2025) has the same address and phone, (520) 792-3715, but different hours (Mon–Thu 9:30 to 3), so the hours need a check on the store's own site, and the editor should swap the `url` to https://www.antigonebooks.com/ once it loads. GayTucson's page says the store was "founded in 1973 as a predominantly lesbian-owned feminist bookstore" and has been "women-run from the start," with Kate, Melissa and Morgan taking over in 2018. If Antigone's own About page says the lesbian-owned history in those words, the note can carry it as history. It stays `bookstore` either way: nothing says the current owners describe it as queer-owned. Local First Arizona calls it "women-owned."
- **Heirloom Farmers Market at Rillito Park — own site and hours.** heirloomfm.org didn't load (permission withdrawn). The sources disagree on hours: Visit Tucson's listing says 9 to 1 in winter and 8 to noon in summer; Tucson Foodie (Oct 2024) says 9 to 1; a vendor calendar (vendorsmap.com) lists 8 to noon every Sunday through Dec 31, 2026. The address 4502 N 1st Ave is from Tucson Foodie and the vendor calendar; Visit Tucson gives "southeast corner of Rillito Park Racetrack." Confirm the switch date and the address on heirloomfm.org, and swap the `url` to it.
- **Homeward Books Collective** — its own Bookshop.org page calls it "a worker owned, leftist bookstore located in Tucson" with "a focus on queer & BIPOC authors," phone (520) 222-7276, and Tucson Love Letter says it hosts drag nights and a queer clothing swap. No address found, and the Instagram (@homeward.books) didn't load. It never says "queer-owned." Worth a second bookstore slot if the editor finds the address on its Instagram or Google listing.
- **Dirty T Tamarindo** — Local First Arizona calls it "an LGBTQ+-owned candy shop" selling Mexican spicy candy in Tucson. That's a third party's word, not the shop's. Need its own site or bio saying so, and an address.
- **A bar on the Loop.** The post-ride bar on the path itself is the gap. Tucson Hop Shop (3230 N Dodge Blvd, on the Rillito) has "bike racks and loaner locks," and Catalina Brewing (6918 N Camino Martin, Marana) has "tools available in case anyone's bike needs a fix," per This Is Tucson (undated). Westbound sits in the Mercado beside Transit Cycles. None fetched on its own page.
- **Bumsteds, Venture-N, Bawker Bawker Cider House, Surly Wench Pub** — Tucson Love Letter (Feb 2026) lists them as places with queer programming (Venture-N as a leather bar with a patio; Surly Wench as an alt and goth bar on Fourth Avenue). Their own sites didn't load. None is listed as queer-owned anywhere I read.
- **Tucson Sentinel, "A bittersweet era: Few gay bars remain in Tucson as acceptance grows"** (tucsonsentinel.com, Dec 2025 by its URL) — 403. Worth reading for which gay bars are still open before the editor adds a second one.
