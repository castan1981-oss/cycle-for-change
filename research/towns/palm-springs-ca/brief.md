## Who it's for

A winter rider — a long weekend or a week between November and April —
flying into PSP (the airport is in town) or driving from LA or Phoenix. Many
come for the Tour de Palm Springs in February. They want flat valley miles,
one big climb (Highway 74, or the Tram road), a group ride to join, and a
small hotel that lets the bike in the room. Palm Springs is a queer town;
that informs the picks (where to stay, where to eat after) and never
headlines the page.
