bookstore, Streetbar) stay, with what they confirmed. Six are new, each read
on its own page this run: the Aerial Tramway (tickets page), the Art Museum's
Architecture and Design Center on the Tour start block (with the main museum

visitor bureau's, not the bar's. The Best Bookstore's own site is a script
that gives the fetcher only its metadata ("Family-owned bookstore with
branches in Palm Springs and San Francisco"), so its hours are still the
city's listing against a directory's. No bike parking confirmed anywhere. No
geocoder page read, so nothing is measured from the brief's centre; positions
are block counts from Palm Canyon at Tahquitz. Seven WebSearch calls (the
session's 12 were split 5 stay / 7 culture), eighteen fetches, two 403s (the

## Why these

- **Palm Springs Aerial Tramway**: the rider who climbed Tramway Road wants to see the top; the one who didn't gets 8,500 feet and snow in February for $37. The tickets page has the hours and the price; the maintenance month is September, not the season a rider comes.
- **Architecture and Design Center**: a block from the Tour start, two hours, free on Thursday evenings. The main museum rides along in the note so the editor has both.
- **Palm Springs Swim Center**: the swim. Opens at 6 a.m. every day, $10, a public pool a rider can spin out in after the ride.
- **VillageFest**: the Thursday night everyone downtown ends up at, six to ten, on the same street the Tour starts on two days later.
- **The Shops at 1345**: one address that is the Uptown Design District in miniature, with hours on its own page, so the guide can name the district without listing twelve shops.
- **Hunters**: the second Arenas Road bar, the dance one, and the one place in town that says queer-owned in its own words.
- **Palm Springs Vinyl Records & Collectibles**: the downtown record store, open every day till 6, a block from the start.
- **The Best Bookstore in Palm Springs**: the independent bookstore downtown, with an LGBTQ section and architecture shelves.
- **Streetbar**: the first gay bar in town, with a patio, open from 10 a.m. The plain answer to "where do I get a drink after the ride."
