## Miles: block

- **The strip reads as progress.** "4,273" and "10,000" are the first two cells, same size, same weight, side by side: the top row of the 2×2 on a phone, cells 1 and 2 on desktop. The second is in rose. That's the raised-and-goal layout, and it says "4,273 toward 10,000" before anyone reads "training miles since June 1" or "The count restarts in 67 days". The live strip had 111 between them. Fix: take the training count out of the strip (it's in the tally line under the header and in `#road`). Then the strip is the year: 10,000 miles in 2027, the restart countdown, group rides, events. Or put a non-miles cell between them.
- **"Mile 4,273 of training." reads as progress** (`build-home.js:261`). It sits under the section's giant 10,000 and under the bike on a painted lane: a mile marker on the road to 10,000. "Mile N of" is the progress construction. On a phone, 4,273 sits on its own line and "of training. The 10,000 start Jan 1." follows, so the eye reads "Mile 4,273 of … 10,000". Fix: use the tally line's existing words, "4,273 training miles since June 1. The 10,000 start Jan 1.", and set them in the log column with "111 rides since June 1", away from the bike and the lane.
- **Passes:** no percentage, fraction or progress bar anywhere. The log strokes are scaled to the longest of the four rides (77.8 mi is full width), not to 10,000. "10,000 miles in 2027." and "Part of the 10,000 miles. Come ride one." are the year, not a count.
