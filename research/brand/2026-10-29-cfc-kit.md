## The blocks and their fixes

**1. Today I rode for. Post 3, Story 3, Story 3b (check 6, photos and dates; check 7, health).**
- **The photo is hard-coded.** `social.today()` always uses `img/today/oct12-gravel-1.jpg` (`social.py:67`). `--for "…"` changes the word but never the picture.
- **"Today" is false on any day but Oct 12.** Under the label "Today I rode for", the post claims the Oct 12 ride is today. On Oct 18 the homepage dropped "Today's ride" for the dated eyebrow ("The ride on Monday, October 12.") for exactly this reason.
- **The label isn't on the site.** "Today I rode for" appears nowhere a visitor can see; it's only the section's name in `today.json`'s note. It also reads "for" twice: "Today I rode for / CYCLE FOR / mental clarity".
- **Health.** "mental clarity" is the mental-health language `today.json`'s own note says the homepage covers with its 988 + Trevor line. Nothing covers it here, and a Story has no caption to carry it.

**Fix:**
- Take the photo from `today.json` (`photo.src`), not a constant.
- Make `--for` take a `--photo` (or drop the photo when no new one is given).
- Use the homepage's eyebrow, built from the date: "The ride on Monday, October 12." For 3b, use a `--date` that defaults to the build day.
- When the day's word is about the mind, print `lib.HELP` in the field ("If it’s now: 988, call or text, any hour. Trevor Project 1-866-488-7386."). On 3b, always leave room for it.
- While in there: Story 3's crop (`26% 50%`) clips the plate painted in the photo to "chang" at the right edge. Crop it whole or crop it out.

**2. Stat posts and Stories, all six (check 2, honest over impressive).** They print the source and drop **the catch**. The cards print it. Examples:
- **work-19** "89% of straight cyclists surveyed…" loses "The catch: 211 riders who chose to answer."
- **youth-18** "2 in 3 LGBTQ+ students felt unsafe at school…" and "Most queer students go to school bracing for harm." lose "The catch: An advocacy group’s survey, not a random sample."
- **screen-13** loses "The catch: Random-sample panels."

The public piece shouldn't be less honest than the handout, and all three posts have an empty band where it fits.

**Fix:** print `s["caveat"]` under the source line in `social.stat()` (`social.py:94`), as `printkit.cards()` does.

**3. Ride with me. Post 5 and Story 5 (check 6).**
- **A place claim the photo doesn't support.** The photo is `robert-desert`, Paradise Valley, AZ · Sep 2025. With no caption, it sits straight under "Tour de Palm Springs / Palm Springs, CA · road", so it reads as Palm Springs. The homepage runs the same photo in `#ride` with its caption under it.
- **New copy in his voice.** "All 13 of my 2027 rides are on the site." isn't a site line (`social.py:133`). The site says "All my 2027 rides, with sign-up links" (build-home.js:344), and the count as "13, so far".

**Fix:**
- Put the registry caption, "Paradise Valley, AZ · Sep 2025", in the field under the photo. Use the site's caption style: left edge, bold, gunmetal.
- Swap the line for "All my 2027 rides, with sign-up links."

**4. The count. Post 8 and Story 8 (check 6).**
- **The photo is outside the kit's own sources.** It's `img/sign-print-4.jpg`, the Pass 32 hero, which is no longer on the site. It isn't in the registry or `#today`, the only sources the kit's section of CLAUDE.md allows.
- **No caption on record.** It has no place · month anywhere. CLAUDE.md only says "the canal path".
- **It shows processing.** At post size the path and the water in the lower left show smeared, painterly texture. It reads as a filter, which the site doesn't allow on a photo.

**Fix:** use a registry photo of him that the kit doesn't already use. Two options:
- `robert-selfie-camelback` (Paradise Valley, AZ · Oct 2025)
- `robert-boise` (Boise, ID · May 2025)

Or get Robert's original export of the canal selfie and register it with its place · month.

**6. The 10K sticker (checks 3 and 5).**
- **It's a new lockup.** The stencil is followed by 10K on the blank's plate, so the sticker reads "CYCLE FOR 10K".
- **It has no precedent.** 10K has only ever stood alone (KIT-LOCK: "Wearable on kit"). The plate has only ever carried a who or a why: "change", "me", the day's word, "the Center", "SFAF", a visitor's answer.
- **It changes the story.** "Cycle for 10K" says he rides for the number. The site's story (§1) is that he rides because it changed him.

**Fix:** pick one:

- Keep this lockup only if Robert OKs it.

## The eight checks, across the kit

1. **Words: pass.**
   - No banned words. Nothing from the never-list: no pledge, no "for queer communities", no 7,500 or Ironman, no age, no sober-time count, nothing about a relapse.
   - "Recovery" appears only in the event name Recovery Ride and in two population stats (survival-13 and survival-17), never about Robert.
   - **Miles:** never a fraction, never a percentage, never set against 10,000. The count is one number with season.js's words. "The 10,000 start Jan 1." and "Part of the 10,000 miles." are both the site's own lines.
2. **Voice: block on two counts.**
   - The catch is missing on the stat pieces (fix 2).
   - New first-person copy on post 5 (fix 3).
   - Everything else is short, plain and the site's own (see the table below).
   - There are no research notes on any piece.
3. **Lane Paint: block on one piece** (the 10K sticker, fix 6). The rest pass:
   - **Stencil:** every one is chrome.js's paths at stroke 16. It only ever spells CYCLE FOR. The stacked version is the share card's (`og-cfc.png`).
   - **The bar and rose:** every bar is the blank, smeared rose on light grounds and powder rose on dark. Rose appears only on the blank, the plates (the date on post 5, "the Center" and "SFAF", the day's word) and nowhere else.
   - **Type:** Overpass only (pdffonts confirms it in every PDF), labels in sentence case, no buttons.
   - **Shapes:** square corners, hairlines only, no shadows, pills, gradients or grain.
   - **Photos:** each sits above a solid field, and nothing is laid over a picture. The reel overlays sit on their own flat tar blocks, as the Fruit Loop kit's did.
4. **The banned look: pass.**
   - No serif, mono or tracked caps.
   - The middots are row separators in sentence-case Overpass, as on the site's rows.
   - No wheel, no "10000", no `///`, and nothing of Claude's default look.
5. **Too polished: pass**, with the 10K sticker blocked under fix 6.
   - The photo pieces are his: his photos, the plate with his word, his standfirst on the flyer, his own 2027 list.
   - The stat posts are the nearest thing to any account's stat cards. The stencil is what holds them, and the catch (fix 2) is what makes them read as one honest person.
6. **Photos: block on three pieces** (fixes 1, 3 and 4).
   - **No cars.** I found no vehicle in `robert-camelback`, `robert-desert`, `oct12-gravel-1` or `sign-print-4`, full frame or mid-ground cropped.
   - **Only Robert** is recognisable.
7. **Health: block on Story 3 / 3b and 10 cards** (fixes 1 and 5).
   - The three stat posts' stats are `mh: false` and aren't mental health.
   - The `mh: true` cards carry the site's own line: "If it’s now: 988, call or text, any hour. Trevor Project 1-866-488-7386."
8. **Two actions: pass.**
   - The flyer has two (Find a group ride, Ride with me).
   - Every other piece has one or none. The URL and handle footers don't count.

## Notes (not blocks)

1. **Crop, a read, not a rule.**
   - Post 1 (`50% 34%`) and post 5 (`50% 30%`) both end the frame on the bib crotch, bottom centre, right above the sign.
   - The flyer ends at the waist, and the Stories run down to the bike; either reads better on a phone grid.
   - Robert's call, for the photo editor.
2. **Ride with me.** Until Dec 11 the site's Ride with me is the Fruit Loop. Post 5 says Tour de Palm Springs. That's right for a Lane Paint piece (the Fruit Loop lives in chalk), but post it after Dec 11 or not beside Fruit Loop posts.
3. **Reel 2 passes on one condition.** It goes on that day's own footage, with the day's word passed through `--for`, and a mind word puts 988 + Trevor in the reel's caption (`instagram-specialist`: the crisis line goes in the caption or the last card).
4. **The count is baked at build time.** 4,273 matches `data/tally.json` (4,273.1, last ride Oct 25), so this run may have used the fallback. Rebuild post 8, Story 8 and reel 3 the day they go out.
5. **Flyer and poster.** If you want exact site wording:

   - Poster: the QR label could be the same line.

7. **work-19, for the fact desk.**
   - The paper is "Drafting behind LGB: Transgender athletes in the sport of cycling". Its point is that trans riders lag behind LGB acceptance.
   - The why line "The peloton is more ready for out riders than many fear." stretches an LGB-only number to all out riders.

   - The greyed rows on post 6 are at 62% opacity on concrete.
9. **The `#today` photo has its own CYCLE FOR "change" in the gravel**, above the post's sign with a different word. It's his photo and it runs on the homepage the same way, so it's not a block. Two signs on one piece is the thing to watch.
