# The Brand Family Playbook (Robert, Oct 27, 2026)

Robert sent "Cycle_for_Change_Brand_Family_Playbook_Refined.pdf" (16 pages, made with ChatGPT, "Art direction for Robert + Claude").
It is not committed (it carries the AI flyer below). What we took from it, and where it runs into the house rules.

## Taken
- Three brands, one family: Cycle for Change (the platform: premium, cinematic, emotional), the Fruit Loop (the gathering:
  hand-drawn, joyful, irreverent), Queers on Bikes (the invitation: human, participatory, global). Kept as separate campaigns.
- "Nobody gets dropped" is the promise. "Queer is who I ride with, never why I ride."
- The Fruit Loop is chalk + photo: full rainbow and scribble, bold lettering, playful marks, the red-dress photo.
- Real photos only, credited and with permission; no generated rider imagery; written permission for likenesses.
- Only promise photo or story intake where the site really takes it (`/cyclefor/` takes clips with the OK, not written stories).

## Where it trips
- Its own model flyer (pages 1, 5, 9) breaks its no-generated-rider rule: Robert's face is regenerated, his lower legs and
  shoes invented (his photo stops at the socks), the bikes and parking lot behind him swapped. It also says "Hosted by",
  which Robert cut the same day, and never says free or 18 and up. The chalk rebuild (`tools/fruitloop-chalk/`) uses his
  real photo with only the background darkened.
- Page 7's swatches say they are "not a replacement for official brand tokens"; the site stays Lane Paint (frozen to Dec 1, 2027).
- Page 12 proposes a new nav (The ride · The story · Fruit Loop · Cycle for) and a homepage line, "Every mile means something."
  That is a site-wide change for Robert to decide; the proposal on the table is two homepage doors (the Fruit Loop, Queers on
  Bikes → `/cyclefor/`) inside the current nav.
- It names "the accompanying markdown" as the source of truth for implementation; that file never came.
- "Needed": real crosswalk photos and permissioned rider stories before the final campaign ships.

## Robert's calls the same afternoon
- The flyer drops "free", "every Friday" and "18 and up"; it shows the miles and the pace (14 flat miles, 10–12 mph).
