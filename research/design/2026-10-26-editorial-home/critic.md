## Ten things that make AFTER look cheap, busy, templated or hard to use (worst first)

**1. Phone hero: the seams cut through the type, three clips fight each other, and the still is a smear.**
Where: after/375se-first. A dark seam at y≈220 runs through the top of CYCLE (the letters start at ≈197), and a second
at y≈445 splits "I'd like to say I ride for" from "everyone else". In after/390-first the second seam lies right under
"on the bike." (y≈563). In after-reduced/390-first the top band is a motion-blurred close-up of a jersey, which is
what a reduced-motion phone (or an iPhone in Low Power Mode) shows. In most frames on the phone sheet his face isn't
readable.
Fix: under 760px, show one clip at full height with no seams, or a still. The poster should be a sharp frame with his
face in the top 40%; the mint-jersey frame in after/390-00 is the right one. If the bands have to stay, no type may
cross a seam: put the stencil and both lines inside the bottom band and keep the top two bands picture only.

**2. Desktop hero: the sign straddles both seams and lands on his body.**
Where: after/1440-first. CYCLE crosses the seam at x≈465, half on sky and mint jersey, half on pink mural. FOR sits
across his hips in the middle panel. The rose bar crosses the second seam at x≈972. The intro line wraps to an
orphaned "on the bike." (y 376). In after/1440-00 the middle tunnel panel is visibly soft at full bleed.
Fix: put the seams on grid lines and keep the sign inside one panel. Make panel 1 columns 1–6 (x 96–700) and scale the
stencil to fit it (about 560px wide). Or go back to a flat bruise text panel on columns 1–6 with the film on 7–12;
before/1440-first was the calmer and stronger composition. Widen the intro box so the line sets on one line at
≥1024, or balance it.

**3. It says everything two or three times.**
Where: 10,000 appears in the hero line, the stat band (after/1440-00 y 1015), a 280px rose numeral (after/1440-01
y 1520–1790), "The 10,000 start Jan 1." (after/1440-02 y 697) and "Part of the 10,000 miles" (y 1155). 4,273 appears in
the stat band and again as "Mile 4,273" (after/1440-02 x 838 y 600). 1,423 appears in the stat band, as a 130px numeral
and in the table (after/1440-04 y 1330 and y 1375). 640 appears twice. "Restarts in 67 days", "start Jan 1" and
"starts over on January 1" all fall within two screens. This padding is most of why the phone page is a third longer.
Fix (cuts only): keep one band of numbers.
- Cut the "Mile 4,273 / of training…" block.
- Cut either the stat-band 10,000 or the giant one. Keep the giant one; it's the better move.
- Cut the Group rides row from the 05 table.
- Keep one "count restarts" sentence.

**4. The numbered sections are a template tic, and they label the wrong things.**
Where: "01" has no label. "02 From the road" now sits over the giant 10,000 instead of the rides it named before.
Then come "03 13 rides", "04" with no label, "05 Group rides" and "06 Read". How this works, the Oct 12 ride, Ride with
me and the closing have no number at all. The rules differ too: 01 stops at x 600, 05 at x 708, and 03, 04 and 06 run
to 1344 (after/1440-00, -02, -03, -04).
Fix: cut the numbers. Keep the existing labels at 13px/700 over the section they name, which puts "From the road" back
over "The last rides." Use one rule length (the full grid width) or no rule.

**5. The line-drawn bicycle is clip art.**
Where: after/1440-02 x 860–1320 y 265–520, and after/390-03 y 715–860. It's a generic frame with the saddle floating
off the post, a kickstand-like stub under the chainring and doubled wheel rings, sitting on rose dashes. Any brand
could have it.
Fix: cut it. That column is where "Watch the last ride", his own 4:5 footage, used to sit (before/1440-01 x 841–1344).
Put his footage back there, or let the list run full width with the mile count beside it.

**6. Rose is used in bits, everywhere, so it no longer means the blank.**
Where: in the year list, 13 solid rose date tiles, two rose plates and 13 rose map squares (after/1440-02, -03,
after/390-04). All four ride bars are now rose; before, only the newest was (before/1440-01 y 1355). On top of that:
a 280px rose 10,000, rose chips, rose dashes three times, and a rose underline on the closing input.
Fix:
- Date tiles in plaster with an ink hairline and ink type; projected dates in gunmetal or dashed.
- Rose fill only on his two org rides, which is the rule the plates already follow.
- Gunmetal bars, with only the newest in rose.
- The giant 10,000 in plaster.

**7. The new textures aren't finished.**
- **Torn tabs.** On plaster grounds the tab is the same colour as the ground, so all you see is a caption 14px off its
  photo (after/1440-01: "Phoenix · Oct 2026" at x 534, photo at x 520; after/390-02: x 30 against a 16px margin). On
  concrete, the tear reads at 1x as a grey smudge under the picture (after/1440-04 x 96–305 y 930–968). That's the
  "sloppy" and "unfinished" Robert has already named.
- **Grain.** It shows at 1x as separate white specks, like dust on the glass, including behind 14–15px text
  (after/390-03 y 500–560, after/1440-05 y 400–1050). It isn't restrained.
- **Dashes.** They're the most ownable new move, but the run starts 8px from the left edge and ends 25px short of the
  right (after/1440-00 y 927), and the motif is used three times.
- **Ride bars.** The ragged painted ends (after/1440-02 y 320–600) read as tape.

Fix:
- **Tabs:** either cut them and set captions flush with the photo's left edge, or use one tab that always contrasts
  (plaster on concrete, concrete on plaster), with its left edge on the photo's left edge and a crisp 2–3px deckle
  instead of a blur.
- **Grain:** finer and fainter (1px, monochrome, about 3% opacity), felt rather than seen, and never behind body text.
- **Dashes:** bleed both ends equally, and use them once (the top of the stat band) or twice at most.
- **Ride bars:** square ends.

**8. Lines that don't line up.**
- In the stat band, 4,273 and 10,000 sit on a baseline at y≈1040 while 1,423 and 640 sit at y≈1072, because only the
  last two carry icons. Their captions fall on two different lines as well (after/1440-00 y 990–1110).
- Arrows touch their words: "worldwide→", "2027→", "video→" (after/1440-00, after/1440-01 y 1023, after/390-00,
  after/390-02).
- The body text in How this works' third column starts 15px lower than the other two (after/1440-01, y 456 against
  441). SFAF in the orgs section does the same (after/1440-03, y 1225 against 1194).
- The map's "Phoenix" label is clipped to "oenix" by marker 1 (after/1440-02 x 195 y 1563).
- The phone fact table breaks "Apr 23– / 25, 2027" across two lines (after/390-06 y 1008–1035).
- "queer- / serving" splits at the hyphen at display size (after/1440-03 y 1680).

Fix:
- Give every stat a fixed 24px icon slot, or none, and put all four numerals on one baseline.
- Use the `.link` gap before every arrow.
- Start column bodies on one line (subgrid rows, or a two-line heading box).
- Move the Phoenix label clear of the markers.
- Set date ranges `white-space: nowrap`, and stack label over value under 480px.
- Use `hyphens: none` and balance on the statement.

**9. Desktop dead zones and a doubled picture.**
Where: in after/1440-03 (x 96–830 y 0–640) the left half is empty for a whole screen while the year list runs on alone
(the map stops at y≈1700 of after/1440-02). In after/1440-01 (x 96–456 y 1053–1384) the left column is empty under the
dark box. The Oct 12 photo (bottom at y 1087) and film (bottom at y 1230) don't share a bottom edge, and both show the
same wall in the same kit.
Fix: make the map `position: sticky; top: 104px` inside its section so it travels beside the list, or put a
full-width map over a two-column list. In #today, put one picture beside the words (the selfie with the drawn route)
and move the 3 s film under the words in the left column, with the bottoms aligned.

**10. Headings that invert, a blank that shrank, and a page that ends on two signs.**
- "Ride with me." is a 40px line between a 13px kicker and a 64px "April 23–25, I cycle for the Center"

Fix:

- Shrink the footer stencil to the header-size lockup, so the page ends on one sign.

## Against the bar

**Finish (Assos, MAAP, Pas Normal, Rapha).** The after reaches for MAAP's energy and gets the motion, but not the
edit. A MAAP hero runs one clip, one line and one button, and the type never crosses a cut. Assos's precision is where
it fails hardest: four stats on two baselines, captions that miss their photos by 14px, a clipped map label. Pas
Normal would cut half the motifs (numbers, specks, tabs, the bike drawing). Rapha would keep section 01 and the orgs
statement. Section 01 is the one place it already looks like a house.

**Clarity (Specialized, Tesla).** Tesla says a number once, huge, and moves on. This page says 10,000 five times and
1,423 three times, and it's a third longer on a phone. The before was closer to product clarity.

**One man's site, not a template.** His photos and his film are more present than ever, which is the right
direction. The dressing around them (numbered sections, visible grain, torn tabs, a stock bicycle) is the editorial
template kit, and that's the part Robert would call "could be anyone's". The direction asked for softer. The after is
louder than the before.
