## Five things that make it look cheap or hard to use, worst first

1. **"How this works." is a landing-page template.** 1440-screen-01, y 265–540: a hairline, a small head, big 1 / 2 / 3 and two grey lines each, in three columns. At 390 it's the same block stacked (390-screen-01, y 755–1550). It runs straight into "It always says cycling." on the same concrete, so the story opens as step four.
2. **The phone film is stranded.** 390-screen-05, y 128–590: the `#today` film fills the right half (x 330–748), with bare concrete on the left and the play disc over his torso. It looks like a desktop grid that never collapsed.
3. **The first screen looks framed, not full.** 390-first, y 0–126: a tar slab holding only the menu. 1440-first: five links centred in a black bar with both ends empty, and a dark seam at x≈1025 between the panels. It reads as a player inside a page.
4. **The proof room has a hole.** 1440-screen-03, y 712, and 390-screen-03, y 1413: the fourth "last ride" is named "—". Same kind of slip: the ride bar cuts off at "Palm Spring…" (390-screen-01, y 1645).
5. **Two dead zones at 1440.** 1440-screen-02, x 96–390: the shadow-on-road photo is a 294 px square stranded under the story, with 340 px of empty concrete before the portrait. 1440-screen-03, x 96–560, y 410–790: nothing under "111" while the log fills the right.

## Three things that already work

1. **The phone first screen** (390-first): his face clear in the top half, then the sign, who and what, one tile, two links and pause, all inside 844 points.
2. **The plate as the blank:** "for [the Center]" (1440-screen-08) and "CYCLE FOR [mental clarity]" (390-screen-04). Rose is always the word that changes.
3. **The rooms and the ending:** big calm fields that alternate. 1440-screen-10, the stencil full width on bruise over the sign's input, is the best screen on the page.

## Against the bar

The rooms and the stencil at scale come close to MAAP's finish. The first screen is as clear as Specialized's. It is plainly one man's site. What keeps it from a fashion house: snapshot photography in fashion-house layouts, and template blocks between the good rooms (the 1-2-3, the footer columns, thirteen boxed date badges on a phone).

## The five asked

- **Fashion-editorial sophistication: 3.** The scale and rooms are there. The 1-2-3 and the repeated front-on poses keep it a well-made personal site.
- **Story and "The ride on Monday…" composition: 3.** Desktop `#today` is the best of it: the stencil-and-plate head, the photo and film aligned at the bottom. The story's thumbnail and the phone's half-width film undo it.
- **Phone rhythm: 4.** The rooms alternate, about one opener per screen. It breaks where How this works runs into the story, and it ends on a footer two screens long.
- **Graphics: 4.** The log strokes measure something, the map is quiet, the org marks are thin. The dashes over the strip's numbers come nearest to decoration.
- **Still a template (5 = none): 2.** How this works is the worst, then the footer and the boxed date rows.
