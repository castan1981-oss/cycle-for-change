# Critic, blind read 2: three homepage sets (P, Q, R)

Read from screenshots only: `scratchpad/pass/blindP|Q|R/` (390 first screen and 390-screen-NN; 1440 first screen and 1440-screen-NN; the four overview sheets). I looked at every screen of every set at both widths. I read no brief, no notes and no code. The ten lines are the score in `research/design/STUDIO.md`. At 390, line 7 reads "the width is used on purpose". At 1440, line 8 reads "nothing sits in the way".

**How the shots were taken.** All three sets were taken one scrolled screen at a time. Q was re-shot that way after the first read, which used a full-page capture where the sticky header and the bar couldn't show. I re-read every re-shot Q screen at both widths. Q's content is identical to the full-page capture. On the phone, Q's header and Ride with me bar behave the way P's do: the bar sits at the foot and drops out while the #ride section is on screen (`390-screen-09`). At 1440, the header brings back the mark and RIDE WITH ME once the film scrolls under it (`1440-screen-01`), and no Q screen shows the bar (`1440-screen-01` to `-11`). No Q number moved, so the table keeps one Q column per width. R's 1440 tiles show the bar on every screen. P's and Q's show none.

P and Q are almost the same page. They differ in one section: "The ride on Monday, October 12." R is a different arrangement. It has marks in How this works and three rose plates under the story, #today sits before #road, the map pins are round, the hero has one quiet link, and the bar shows at 1440.

## 2. What holds each set back (five each, worst first)

### P
1. **`390-screen-05`**: the #today film drops below the wide photo. It sits half-width on the right, and its caption "Phoenix · Oct 2026" sits alone at the bottom-left. The left half above it is empty concrete. It reads as a grid that failed to stack.
2. **`1440-screen-04`**: in "The ride on Monday, October 12." the words start at x≈838. The big photo ends at 920 and the film starts at 944. That's three edges in one spread, so the words float between columns.
3. **`1440-screen-08`**: the #ride facts are three narrow columns under a 64 px heading. "200+ miles, / Apr 23–25, 2027" and "You raise it on the / Center's page" break mid-phrase, and the money line looks squeezed.
4. **`390-screen-06`**: the orgs crisis line is "988 call or text, any hour · Trevor Project", then a blank line's worth of leading, then "1-866-488-7386 or text START to 678-678". The Trevor Project's name and its number read as two separate items.
5. **`1440-screen-09`** and **`390-screen-12`/`-13`**: from Find a group ride down, the page turns into a links page. The search field sits at the top of the right half with ~180 px of empty plaster under it. Field notes are four text-only columns, and the phone footer is two screens of single links.

### Q (re-read on the re-shot screens)
1. **`390-screen-04`**: "What do you cycle for? Say it on video" wraps to two lines in the narrow left column, and its arrow hangs at the right edge between them. The film beside it is ~280 px wide, the smallest picture on the phone page: a thumbnail next to type.
2. **`1440-screen-08`**: the same squeezed three-column #ride facts as P ("200+ miles, / Apr 23–25, 2027"; "You raise it on the / Center's page").
3. **`390-screen-06`**: the same crisis-line gap as P ("988 call or text, any hour · Trevor Project", a blank line, then "1-866-488-7386 or text START to 678-678"). The Trevor Project's name and its number come apart. The footer repeats it the same way (`390-screen-13`).

### R
1. **`1440-screen-05`, `-06`, `-01`, `-02`**: the Ride with me bar floats over content at 1440. It covers row 9's line, the SFAF heading, the shadow photo and the LOVE photo, while the header's RIDE WITH ME sits right above. It's said twice, and it's in the way.

## 4. Against the bar
The top two screens are at the bar: his own film, the stencil, one line of his and one button. That's closer to MAAP's restraint than most charity sites get, and it's plainly one man's. Below the 10,000 it becomes a well-made product site: lists, a search field, four columns of text links and a long footer. That's Specialized-grade clarity, not Assos-grade finish. Q carries the finish furthest down the page. P breaks it in the #today spread. R breaks it earliest, with a feature-icon row, round pins, decorative plates and a bar sitting on the content.
