## How it was run

- **What was measured:** `design/2026-10-27-cadence-final` at `cbbc7224ed`, as built in `cfc-site/` (`index.html` written
  10:43, `home.css` 10:33, `chrome.css` 10:29, `home.js` 10:19). The tree was clean. Nothing on the site was changed.
  - For side-by-side numbers the before report didn't record (the stencil's position at 390, 430 and 1440; `#why`'s
    blocks; the /resources/ door), the before build was unpacked read-only from `c796bdf7e2` into the scratch folder
    (`git archive`) and measured the same way.
- **Same setup as before:** in-process `http.server` started by the same Python script that drives Playwright; `/api/*`
  and `/.netlify/*` answer 404, so the tally line shows "—" and the baked 4,273 stays; every request leaving 127.0.0.1 is
  blocked (none were attempted); no Strava endpoint was called. Reduced motion on, with a motion-on run for the films and
  layout shift. Phone 390×844, `is_mobile`, touch, 2x; desktop 1440×900; spot checks 375×667, 360×640, 430×932.

  and clicked on desktop. Screens = scrollY ÷ viewport height, summed across the path, until the target is whole on
  screen and not under the Ride with me bar. Smallest target = the box, with the real hit area (`elementFromPoint`) in
  brackets.

  `display:none`). Added: `telall.py`, `barwidths.py`, `films.py`, `todaypoll.py`, `tabwalk.py`, `facefilm.py`, `why.py`,
  `resdoor.py`.
