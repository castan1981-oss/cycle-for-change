## Five things that make it look cheap or hard to use

1. **Dead right halves at 1440.** On 1440-screen-04 the last-rides log stops at x≈814, leaving 530px of bruise empty. On 1440-screen-05 the map key sits bottom-right under about 400px of nothing. On 1440-screen-07 the queer line runs half-width beside empty patina. On 1440-screen-08 the search box hangs below "Find a group ride" and lines up with nothing.
2. **The Ride with me bar covers the page.** On 1440-screen-05 it cuts off "Toronto to Montr…", and on 1440-screen-06 it sits on "San Francisco AIDS Foundation". From 1440-screen-01 on, it repeats the header's RIDE WITH ME on every screen.
3. **Two template sections.** "How this works." (1440-screen-01, 390-screen-01) is an icon, a bold line and a grey sentence, three across: any SaaS page. "Field notes, guides, the journal." (1440-screen-09) is four text-only teasers, a footer before the footer.
4. **The today section falls apart.** At 1440 (1440-screen-02/03) the film is a thumbnail with its own Play/0:03 strip, beside an eight-column photo. On the phone (390-screen-03/04) there are 120–160px gaps between label, sign, line, link and photo, as much air as between sections. Then one 3-second clip gets two play controls. The label is fifteen days old.
5. **Small breaks.** Text smears through the frosted header (390-screen-02, "says cycling."). On the phone the search is an empty box over an equal ghost box (390-screen-11). The phone footer runs two screens, with the Read column ending halfway down (390-screen-12…14).

## Three things that work. Keep them.

1. The first screen, both sizes: two film panels with one face at a time, the stencil at full scale, one plaster tile (1440-first, 390-first).
2. The 10,000 room and the closing sign: real scale in real dark fields (1440-screen-03, -09; 390-screen-12).
3. The 2027 rides: a drawn map, numbered dots, date blocks dashed when the date is projected. Custom and honest (1440-screen-05, 390-screen-06).

## Against the bar

The hero and the dark rooms are finished to Pas Normal's level. The middle is a tidy content site: medium heads on concrete, icon rows, teasers. The first screen is as clear as Specialized. After that the phone runs fifteen screens. It is one man's site, apart from item 3.

## The five directions

- Fashion-editorial sophistication: **3**. The hero and the 10,000 room get there. The middle doesn't.
- Story and today composition: **3**. The big head with two staggered photos works (1440-screen-01/02). Today's thumbnail and loose stack don't.
- Phone rhythm: **2**. Concrete screens, then two full-bleed wall portraits back to back (390-screen-04), a 13-row list and a two-screen footer. Groups are spaced as loosely inside as between.
- Graphics, custom and restrained: **4**. The dashes sit on what they mark, and the map and marks are drawn clean. Three rose plates in a row read as tags (390-screen-02).
- Template left: **3**. How this works, Read and the search box could be anyone's.
